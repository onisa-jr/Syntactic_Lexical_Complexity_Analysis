Are you going shopping for enjoyment or only for what you need? These days, many people do online shopping or go to the mall. Shopping may be for important items like soap or milk, but sometimes we buy candy only because we feel bored. This essay talks about shopping for fun reasons.

In the past, people only did shopping when they had to. They went to the market only for food or something to wear. But now, we have big stores and online websites where we can choose many items like colorful hats or toys that are not needed. Sometimes people only walk around the store and do not buy anything, just to look and feel good. This is like an activity to relax after a stressful day. Many people also post about shopping on Instagram or other apps to show what they see or try.

Another point is that shopping does affect mood and can give more joy. If you buy one small gift for yourself, maybe you feel better. This is why not all people think shopping is only for necessities. It can be a way for self-care and to improve your emotions. Even if you do not buy, the looking is fun too.

But some people do not think the same. They believe shopping for fun is a waste of budget and time. They want every bit of money to be used only for important things. This opinion is also valid and has reasons.

To finish, people have different thoughts about shopping. Some like to buy for enjoyment, and some only for need. It is a person’s choice how they want to spend time and money. Shopping can be an experience for happiness or only for necessity.
