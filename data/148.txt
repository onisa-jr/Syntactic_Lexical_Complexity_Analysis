Shopping is a hobby. For some, it is buying what is necessary, but for some people it is a hobby or an entertainment. However, for me, it is an entertainment. I am going to talk in this essay about the joy of following the latest.

Buying stuff, even if you do not need it, is very exciting and fun. The joy it brings is not replaceable. Getting ready to go is also a plus. Sometimes, it is not about buying stuff; it is also about seeing people and meeting new people who have the same passion as you.

Sometimes, I do not end up buying anything. But seeing the trends and the new stuff is what I really like. Following fashion and seeing what is new makes me feel elegant. Following fashion also makes me go out of the house and meet friends, which I do not often do.

However, some people might say that buying only what is necessary is mindful, and not wasting money is the right thing to do. But in my opinion, I think what is important at the end of the day is my feeling, and being happy is also important. It is not always the right thing to do, but I do not think you always have to think about the right thing. It is okay to spoil yourself.

Window shopping is also a thing. By watching what the shops have, you can later spend the extra money you have, or even save up for it. I, myself, if I want to buy something expensive, I save up for it.

In conclusion, I think the joy and following the latest fashion is the entertainment that shopping brings me.
