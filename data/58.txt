Some people might think shopping is just a task that has to be done, rather than a fun little activity to do. Some others might even find it exhausting for them. Perhaps it is because they always go to the same places to shop. Regardless of that, some people prefer shopping for a lot of things, such as making them feel better, trying new stuff, and socializing.

You might be wondering, “How can shopping make people feel better?” Think about it this way: a person is having a bad day and wants to forget about it. Shopping can be like a treat to help you get rid of the negative emotions and make you feel good about yourself.

Trying new clothes feels nice and good, although some might overdo it. However, it helps people mentally a lot. Trying different and new clothes, foods, or even games can make your day better.

Shopping can also help with socializing and hanging out with friends. Doing it with other people is always better. It can even help you to get to know them and their tastes better. People might still disagree and deny that shopping is a fun activity, but everyone knows shopping can help with a lot of things, and it should not be just for necessary stuff. For example, going to the mall with your cousin or your classmates can turn into a whole adventure. Some people like to walk around and look at things, even if they do not buy them.

Another reason is that shopping can be a way to celebrate something, like passing a test or getting a new job. People enjoy rewards, and shopping can be one of them.

Even people who say they dislike it still end up buying stuff they like when they go out.
