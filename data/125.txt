In this life, we are doing a lot of things. Things that are important in our lives. Did you ever wonder: what if I enjoy myself while I am doing my important things? This essay argues about shopping. Is it just buying what is necessary, or is it for entertainment?

First and foremost, doing important things while enjoying them will leave a beautiful memory. For example, shopping with friends or with members of your family. It is more like two birds in a tree. You will enjoy yourself and buy what is necessary. Another example is that you will buy faster because time will go fast. It is all about making beautiful memories. In addition, when you walk in the stores with someone you like, you will feel better. You could laugh and talk while picking up the things you need.

However, other people think shopping is just about buying what is necessary. There is no fun in doing this. You will get bored and tired, and the time will go really slowly. Sometimes, you need someone to ask about their opinion. Sometimes, you need somebody to talk with.

Furthermore, I understand if people want to buy only what is necessary, because some of them do not want to spend a lot of money. If they go with anyone, they will buy more. However, if people know what they really want, they will not buy more.

To sum up, try a new experience and bring someone with you. You will notice the difference, and you will enjoy it. Also, you will have fun, buy what is necessary, and make memories.
