When it comes to shopping, people think that it can be a source of entertainment, while some think it is just done to get what is needed. Agreements on both of the ideas were stated, but here is my personal opinion.

While some take shopping seriously and set a specific amount to spend, and schedule a day to finish shopping for essential needs like groceries, buying new clothes for events, or personal care, others find it a form of entertainment. I am one of them. There is no better feeling than spoiling yourself with a trip to a makeup store, or buying a cute outfit or a designer bag. Walking around the mall and exploring all the stores while sipping coffee is one of the best feelings ever.

Shopping for entertainment and spending money on things that are not necessary may be considered by some as a waste of money. However, it can be an investment in your mental state. I think everyone gets excited when buying something they really love, whether it is clothes, cars, makeup, shoes, bags, or anything a person could enjoy, just like when you buy a toy for a child.

In conclusion, I totally agree with the statement. If spending money on things I will enjoy will help me be happy, I will do it. After all, there is no better reason to keep the money and hide it. Not everyone could agree, but everyone does what they wish for, after all.
