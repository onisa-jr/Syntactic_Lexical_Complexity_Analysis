For some people, shopping is necessary, and for others, it is just for fun or entertainment. While I can understand this point of view, I do not agree with this statement.

Some would agree that shopping is for pleasure and fun. It may give you a lovely moment during your hard days. People like to spend their time away from thinking by doing fun things, such as shopping or buying unnecessary things. It is like escaping. There are people who think shopping is an escape from stress, but not all agree, because not everyone feels the same way about it.

I believe most people shop out of need, not just for fun. They buy required items, like groceries and clothes, not because they find shopping fun.

However, shopping just for entertainment can also have problems. It can lead to overspending and focusing too much on material things. People may forget to spend their money wisely. Sometimes, people spend all their time in the mall and forget their responsibilities, which is not always good, because time is valuable.

At the end, while a few see shopping as fun, for most it is simply a practical task to get what is needed, not leisure. The satisfaction comes from acquiring necessary items or things, not from the shopping process itself. Shopping may provide some enjoyment for others, but it remains primarily a functional activity rather than a form of entertainment for most consumers. Shopping too much can cause debt and regret, so people should be careful about how and when they buy things.
