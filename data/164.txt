Shopping is a process in which a person aims to fulfill or buy something they need. For example, perfume, food, shoes, objects, and clothes. This essay will dive into the concept of shopping and why it is a form of entertainment.

Shopping is not just about buying what is necessary, but it is a form of entertainment because, for most people, shopping centers are places that offer joy, excitement, and happiness by combining all their favorite stores in one place. Moreover, for them, shopping is a perfect way to rest away from their reality. For example, when someone is having a bad time, they can go shopping and buy their loved items. This can shift their mood in a short period. Others see it as a lovely place where they can spend their time with their loved ones. In addition, a lot of people have a deep interest in fashion. For them, tracking trends itself is a whole form of entertainment.

Some people would argue that shopping is only about buying what is necessary, and mixing it with entertainment is a waste of both money and time. There is a valid point: buying what is necessary is the main goal of shopping. However, with good control of both time and money, people can experience shopping as entertainment. For example, making a monthly plan and selecting an appropriate budget for shopping can solve this issue. Additionally, according to the free time one has, having two to three days for shopping per month is also another solution.

In conclusion, shopping is a method that meets both people's needs and provides entertainment. This essay explored how the concept of shopping is a form of enjoyment and fun in a new and effective way.
