Some people shop as a form of entertainment. Shopping has many forms depending on a person's needs. Furthermore, people prefer shopping as entertainment to release their negative energy. In this essay, I will be arguing about how people shop as a form of entertainment.

Nowadays, shopping is being used as entertainment. Among different age groups, teenagers like to shop just to release negative energy. People sometimes get bored of traditional shopping, such as shopping only for necessities. Moreover, searching for and collecting some types of items such as clothes, shoes, makeup, and accessories can build someone's personality and self-confidence. Shopping for entertainment is much more enjoyable, especially if it is with friends.

On the one hand, shopping as entertainment can help relieve a lot of negative energy and change someone's mood. Some people see that shopping for entertainment requires a lot of money, but it does not always have to. It depends on seasonal discounts and offers. On the other hand, women often prefer shopping frequently because they see a reflection of themselves in the things they buy, and it also makes them feel more feminine.

Some people may argue that shopping for entertainment is a good choice for changing someone's mood. I understand that some people would disagree and only focus on shopping for necessities, but that does not justify their viewpoint. People need to accept everyone's opinion and interests regarding shopping, whether it is for entertainment or necessity.

In conclusion, shopping can be a great decision to take as a form of entertainment. People who argue against shopping should be more flexible and accept others' points of view.
