There are about millions of people shopping every day. Is this not a surprising fact? People go to the mall not just for necessary things but also for enjoyment. It has been claimed that they can buy food and attend entertaining activities for their children. They can also drink coffee and meet other people. This essay is discussing that people are not going to the mall only for shopping but also to enjoy.

First reason, they go to the mall to meet other people and chat with them. The mall is the best place to meet because there are many facilities in it. There are restaurants, cafes, and such, even when some people say it is not.

Second reason, going to the mall could make people feel better mentally. When buying new clothes or a new necklace, this will mean a lot to them. While other people claim that shopping is boring, it is not.

Third reason, the mall is the best place to eat while shopping. There are many options for restaurants. Other people do not like mall food, but it is the best place to get necessary things and enjoy at the same time.

In conclusion, shopping is not just for necessary things but also to enjoy at the same time. People can meet other people in the mall, and it will make them feel better mentally. And finally, the mall is the best place to eat while shopping.
