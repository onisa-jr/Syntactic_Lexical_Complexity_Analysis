Shopping is something that happens on a daily basis. Every individual, by himself or with a partner or a group, does shopping. Therefore, many people raise the question: is shopping a necessity or a form of entertainment? In this essay, I will discuss how shopping is a necessity and not a form of entertainment.

Shopping can be enjoyable and something that would relieve stress, as many might say. These opinions usually come from younger generations or people who do not shop for their needs. They let their parents do the shopping. I believe their opinion is valid and understood based on their living circumstances. However, it is superficial and shallow.

The necessity of shopping is significant. It is known that shopping is not something entertaining but a needed element. You carefully pick the ingredients in your food or carefully shop for clothes that fit you perfectly. Many people from the middle-class groups view and perceive shopping as a necessity, not something you enjoy. Almost every person agrees that you need to have money so you can actually enjoy the entertaining aspect.

In summary, many opinions are presented, and many agree and disagree. Every opinion is valid and understood. However, some opinions are more logical, such as how shopping is actually a necessary thing in human life. Mankind values it and cannot live without it. There are a lot of aspects that unify the opinion, like how people need clothes, food, drink, and shelter. These make the opinion represented in the essay stronger and more believable.
