Shopping is like a journey, not just a thing you do for food or clothes.  
I believe it is a journey because sometimes you just want to go out and look and walk and not even buy anything, but still feel good afterward.  

Shopping on the weekend or after work has become a routine for many people, not just to get what they need but to feel alive. When you go to the mall or even scroll on your phone looking at items, it is like a show, and you are the main character. The lights, the colors, the music, the sales — all of it is part of the entertainment. Even if you do not buy anything, you still feel like you did something.  

Now let us flip the coin. When someone is shopping for their parent or best friend for a birthday or something, the feeling is warm inside. It is like you are planning a surprise party in your heart. The smile you imagine on their face is worth everything. That is why shopping is more than just things; it is emotions. So yes, entertainment once again.  

But also, not everybody agrees. There are people who say shopping is just spending for no reason and leads to regret later. They say to save your cash for the future. But I think that when you save too much and never buy anything, you miss out. I have done both, and I know.  

So in conclusion, shopping is not only buying stuff, it is entertainment. Yes, I repeat it again, because it is true.
