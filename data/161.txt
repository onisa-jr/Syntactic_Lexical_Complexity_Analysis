Shopping is a very expensive way of entertainment. While some believe that it is just a necessity, shopping can be a way of entertainment for some. Shopping is an obsession to some people, and it shows how it affects them positively. Shopping could affect your mental health, work, and relationships positively.

How could shopping affect your mental health? Going on a Friday to the mall and buying whatever you desire can put you in a good mood, especially for women. Some studies have shown that women are happier when shopping, which will definitely affect their mental health. That eventually will make them work harder for sure.

Does not showing up to work with a new bag make you feel better? Going to work after a weekend full of shopping will make you excited to work harder to make more money. After a long week of hard work, you can reward yourself with a bag or shoes to feel even better. But how is shopping connected to your relationships and social life?

Going shopping with family or friends will only bring you closer as a group. Doing this activity with your close ones will help you know them better, including what they love and what they like. It is also going to help you choose their birthday gifts.

Although some might argue that shopping is only good for necessities, I believe that if shopping is making me feel better, then it is definitely a form of entertainment. As long as you know how to manage your money, you will be able to handle both the necessary and the fun at the same time. I do believe that shopping can be, and is, entertainment.
