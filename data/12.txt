Shopping is considered one of the most common ways for people to entertain themselves. However, some people may disagree on this. In my opinion, shopping can develop communication with friends, family, and shopping as a skill.

Communication with family and friends is one of the most important things. People like to shop with each other, taking advice and their opinions about certain clothes. A study shows that seventy-seven percent of people like to spend time with friends and family during shopping.

A lot of people have learned a skill in fashion and gained experience through shopping. Moreover, while people take advice and opinions from others who are experts in shopping, it increases their skill in fashion. Studies show that people have developed a skill during shopping.

Some claim that shopping for entertainment and communication is just a waste of time. However, they believe it is only a place to buy clothes to wear, not for people to gather. Somehow, shopping can bring people together with friends and family when they do not have time because of their work. It helps to make new relationships and develop a skill. Many people feel happy after they go shopping and see new things, even if they are not buying anything.

In conclusion, shopping is not only about buying clothes. It is about gathering, making new relationships, and learning a skill in fashion. Spending time with your loved ones is all a person needs, and shopping can offer that. Therefore, we should not see shopping only as buying, but as a way to learn, enjoy, and be with people who make us feel happy.
