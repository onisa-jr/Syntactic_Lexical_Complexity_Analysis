Some people think shopping is only for buying important things, but other people think shopping is fun and a good time. In my opinion, shopping is for both. It is necessary and also entertainment. Some people like to go shopping in malls or online just to enjoy themselves. In this essay, I will talk about shopping and whether it is only for needs or also for fun.

Some people believe shopping is just for important things like food or clothes we need. They do not like shopping too much. They think shopping is a waste of time and money if it is not necessary. These people go shopping only when they have to. But other people think shopping is not only for needs but also for fun. They enjoy walking in malls or checking websites. They sometimes buy things they do not need. For them, shopping is like a hobby. They feel happy when they buy something new. Some people shop for others and not only for themselves. Mostly, women like shopping more than men. They enjoy it more and they like to see new things. Men usually shop only for what they need.

In my opinion, I think shopping is fun and also for important things. I like to buy things for my home and for myself. It is something I enjoy and also find useful.

In conclusion, some people think shopping is only for necessities and some think it is fun. Different people have different opinions. Most women like shopping more and men do not enjoy it as much.
