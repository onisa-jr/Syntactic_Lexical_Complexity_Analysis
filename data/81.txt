In our world, shopping has become much easier for people. That might affect people in a good way. Shopping does not have to be only for necessary things. It could be for entertainment or even for business and income.

Shopping nowadays is different from the old days. People tend to shop for entertainment. Shopping for entertainment can be good for people’s mental health. For example, a lot of people with mental health disorders, like depression, take shopping as a fun thing to do. It is one of the best ways to get out of your comfort zone.

Shopping is not only for entertainment. It can also be for business. Some people in these days rely on shopping for their income. For example, personal shoppers on social media rely on shopping for other people as their source of income.

Some people might say that shopping for unnecessary things can be a waste of time and money. However, this waste of time and money can help a lot of people in many different ways, such as improving mental health and providing income. So, it is important for people to shop for unnecessary things from time to time. If things like shopping can help people feel better, then they have to do it, whatever the consequences are.

To sum up, shopping does not have to be only for necessary things like food or clothes. It could be for many other things that are not necessary, like buying something that you already own. We should help people normalize shopping for entertainment. In my opinion, shopping for entertainment is the best way to improve your mental health.
