Shopping is, in a way, a lot of things for people. Some see it as entertainment, and for some, it is a necessity. It depends on the person himself.

Shopping is, in fact, a form of entertainment. This comes in many ways. For example, you just got paid from your job, and you want to spend it on food you have been wanting to try. Or, after a long, hard day, you want to relax, so you go to a spa. Another example is that you want to buy a new bag as a reward for getting a good grade in school. The feeling after you buy something, or just go shopping in general, is a feeling unmatched. Therefore, yes, shopping is not just for the important things, even though they matter. Buying a new television for your home could also be considered entertainment.

However, shopping comes in many forms, as stated before. Therefore, it is also important for many things in everyday life. For example, going shopping for food in the market is essential, because how can you live without it? Another example is buying a couch for your living room, or a table, or whatever it is you may need in that living room. How is someone going to have a proper living room without all of the above? Another example is, let us say you work an office job that requires a laptop, but that laptop breaks down. In this case, you have to go shopping to find a new one, or you may lose your job or will not be able to work as well as before. This is when shopping becomes a necessity, not entertainment.

Lastly, shopping can come in many ways. It can be entertainment or a necessity. It only depends on what or who you may need it for. It can also be both entertainment and a necessity. You could have fun while you are shopping. You can go there with your friend, and you both can shop for what you need for any purpose your heart desires.
