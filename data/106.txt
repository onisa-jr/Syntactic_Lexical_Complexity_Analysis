Shopping is necessary. Buying new things gives a beautiful feeling. In addition, buying things is always a need. In this essay, it will show that shopping is necessary.

First, shopping is fun and leaves a good feeling. It does not mean that it is only for entertainment, but sometimes people need these feelings. On the other hand, some people tend to go shopping when they feel low and sad. It helps to make them feel lighter and better.

Second, shopping is always necessary, whether it is needed or for having fun. Buying things is closely related to motivation. For example, buying things to get ready for the semester or for a trip. It motivates people even more. In addition, it brings extra joy.

Some people may disagree, saying that shopping is just for fun, but having fun is also necessary sometimes. Moreover, some people would still think that shopping is a waste of time and money. However, shopping is never a waste. Also, when someone buys a gift for someone, it is not only shopping. It is a way to show love and care.

Another thing is that shopping can be part of daily routines, like going to buy groceries or school supplies. These are normal but still considered shopping. Even just walking in a store and looking can be relaxing for some. It helps clear the mind.

In conclusion, shopping is different for people, but the most common idea is that it is fun and motivating. Not to mention that it is never a waste and makes everyone feel good.
