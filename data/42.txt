Shopping is a form of entertainment as well as a way of buying necessary things. People usually shop at the mall, where there are stores and shops to just look around and enjoy their time. Shopping is more than buying what you need; it is also a form of entertainment.

When you go to today's mall, you will find many stores that are there for people to relax and entertain themselves. Also, the cafes at the mall are there for people to get their coffee and enjoy some time with whomever they want.

The mall in today’s age is a place for everything. People have plans and meetings with their friends and colleagues, because it is a free place where you can do and wear what you are comfortable in. All malls today are getting bigger and fancier in order to be a place for more than just buying what is necessary.

Some people would argue that malls are only for buying important things. They would say that this place would not be open if it were not for buying and getting what you need. However, when you go to any mall today, you will see how people are treating it as a place for enjoying and having fun. Every mall now has a place for kids and teens to enjoy and have fun.

To conclude, malls at some point in time were a place for buying only what you need. But nowadays, they are more than that. They have a lot of stores, shops, and places for people to relax, have fun, and enjoy their time without buying anything.
