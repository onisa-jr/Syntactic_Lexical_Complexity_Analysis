Many people tend to shop when they feel depressed or emotionally broken, or even when they are happy. When they go shopping, they become relaxed and have the free will to buy whatever they want. For some people, shopping is a form of entertainment rather than just buying what is necessary. People go shopping for many reasons, such as just having fun, thinking of it as a type of gathering with friends, or simply because they do not have anything to do.

Firstly, many girls and women, since the beginning of existence, have cherished their girlhood memories with their friends and loved ones. So they think that if they love shopping and making memories, why not put them together and shop for fun with their friends.

Secondly, lots of people go shopping simply because they are bored and have nothing to do. So they think shopping is a good idea to do in the free time they have, to fill their boredom.

The opposite group of people say that you do not need to spend your money on things just for entertainment. Well-documented articles state that it is helpful psychologically to go shopping because it might help release some stress. It is better to direct your stress into something rather than keeping it inside of you, just like some people go for a run when they are stressed. Large groups of people go shopping for this reason.

In summary, girls in general adore shopping, whether it is with a friend or going alone. Lots of women buy things that other people might consider “unnecessary,” such as jewelry or room decor. Other people actually buy things that are unnecessary just because they are bored and have spare time. Try to go shopping alone or with somebody when you feel sad or happy. It will cheer you up.
