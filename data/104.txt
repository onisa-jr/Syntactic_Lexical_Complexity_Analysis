Many people think shopping is not only for needs but also can be fun sometimes. Since last year, many of them started to go out more and enjoy going shopping after they had stayed at home for too long. Some people go shopping because they really need to buy food or clothes. However, they also go shopping just to walk around and see the stores, or to feel relaxed from work and stress. That is why many people now say shopping is a good way to spend time and have enjoyment.

In some malls, there is music and games for children. So, the family can spend more time in the mall. Also, some people go to eat in the food court and not just to buy things. Some of them do not need anything but still they go, like when they feel bored or want to meet with friends. So, shopping looks like entertainment for many, not only for important things.

Sometimes it is hard to know if you really need something or just want to buy it to feel better. That is what happens when people see discounts or sales. Even if it is not announced on the website or in the store, they feel happy when they find it. Other times, they feel bad if they miss it and feel blame toward themselves.

In my opinion, shopping is not only necessary but also a way to enjoy life, because many people want to feel good even if they do not buy something.
