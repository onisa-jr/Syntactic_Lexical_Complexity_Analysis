There are many people who look at shopping as something that is more than just buying things. Shopping, for them, is a way to feel better or even to pass time. Especially for younger people, shopping is a way to express their feelings and boost their mood. They might not even need something, but they still go out and shop because it gives them a sense of happiness and helps them feel distracted from what is bothering them in life.

For example, if a young person is going through something emotional or feeling down about school or personal issues, they may go shopping to take their mind off of it. Even if they only buy something small, the act of shopping alone can make someone feel better and more in control of what is happening in their life.

Some people might say that using shopping as a way to feel better is not a good idea because it can become addictive. A person can become dependent on shopping and start doing it more than they should. When this happens, it can lead to problems, such as not having enough money to pay for important things or even having to borrow money and get into debt.

Shopping can be a good and fun thing to do, but it is important not to let it control your life. If someone realizes they are shopping too much in order to feel okay, they need to talk to someone and get help before it becomes something worse that can hurt them or others around them.
