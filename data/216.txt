Shopping is the most common activity in the world, and I completely agree with the statement above. I call shopping an activity because it can be a form of entertainment that is also a necessity.

Why is shopping entertaining? Shopping can be very entertaining due to the fact that you are discovering new things, such as clothes, toys, all kinds of food, and much more. Another thing to notice is that when people go to different places, they always want to check the shops and stores in that place.

Malls all around the world are filled with people shopping and having lots of fun. You see children, teenagers, and families everywhere. Most people even take photos with the bags they bought to show others what they purchased and where they got it from.

The biggest proof that people enjoy shopping is that they use their weekends and vacations to enjoy their time shopping. Now, with that being said, shopping is still very important, but that does not mean it cannot be entertaining. Shopping shows your personality, which is why so many people are happy about it.

In conclusion, shopping is obviously necessary, but just because something is necessary, that does not mean it cannot be enjoyed. Looking at important things in too serious and strict a way can take away a lot of joy and fun in your life. Instead, try to enjoy the process. Sometimes, people even shop when they are sad because it helps them feel better.
