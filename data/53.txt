Shopping is where people own a thing by their choice and spend time in very big fun centers. While some people think that shopping is just about buying what is necessary, in this essay the reasons why shopping is a form of entertainment will be explained.

First, people owning material by their choice and, after digging through multiple things, finally getting what they like is entertainment. This is because you do not always get to choose your stuff. For example, school uniforms, your family house, or the car your family chose. So you enjoy shopping because you have the chance to pick what you like.

Second, shopping these days has a lot of ways. For some people, scrolling on their phones and going between store apps, buying things online from other shopping countries at a cheaper price, is entertainment. Moreover, shopping in malls or shopping with personal shoppers is also entertainment for some people, and it is always about preferences that make people have fun.

Finally, shopping for some people is better when it is about buying what is necessary rather than being entertainment, because they think it is a waste of time and money. However, people who agree with that should understand that shopping will take you to a center that has a lot of entertainment sights, while buying what you like and prefer is clearly entertainment.

In conclusion, some people would have fun in those big centers, shopping and buying what they like, not as if it is a way of doing obligatory things.
