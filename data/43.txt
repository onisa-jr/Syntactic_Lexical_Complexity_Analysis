Do people shop only when they need something, or do they do it for fun?

Shopping is not always for important things. Sometimes, people buy things because they want them, not because they need them. For example, someone can go to buy shoes even if they already have many shoes. Some people enjoy buying bags or perfumes just to feel good. In this way, shopping becomes more like entertainment, not just something to do when they need something. Some people even say shopping makes them relax and forget about stress or sadness. I think this is true, and many people feel the same.

Shopping can be helpful for the mind and feelings. People like to go out and see new things in stores or online. Even if they do not buy anything, it still gives them a nice feeling. Also, people who sell things get more money when others shop for fun, so it helps both sides. I believe this is a good reason to say shopping can be more than just for needs.

However, there are people who are against shopping too much. They think it is a waste of money and time. They prefer buying only necessary things like food or clothes when needed. They do not feel happy when shopping.

However, it is not wrong if some people like shopping for fun. Everyone has their own way to feel good. As long as they do not waste too much money, it is fine.

In conclusion, shopping is not only for needs. It can also be something people enjoy and do to feel better.
