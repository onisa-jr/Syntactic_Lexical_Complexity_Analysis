Shopping has been an essence in our lives. We can shop for different things including school, household needs, or even to treat ourselves. In this essay, I will discuss why shopping is not only for fun.

Shopping has always been a part of our weekly list. It is not only a pleasurable activity, but also a way to get things done. Additionally, if shopping is considered only a fun concept, then it means that it is a huge waste of time and money. For me, I dedicate a part of my money to different kinds of shopping. To illustrate, I dedicate part of my money to buy the essentials first, like items on the supermarket list. Therefore, if shopping is only for spending time and having fun, then even the essentials are for fun too, which does not make sense when you shop for your house.

A lot of individuals would disagree with me. They would perceive essential shopping as a fun activity to do, even if it is not shopping to treat oneself. However, there is a difference between shopping just to get things done, which is always a burden and challenging. Therefore, not all kinds of shopping are fun. Secondly, if shopping is a fun activity, then people will start to see it as a way to spend their time, which may lead to spending money in the wrong way.

To conclude, shopping is part of our list of tasks to get things done. To perceive it as a fun way to spend time or anything else may lead to unwanted results. People should see the difference between fun and essentials.
