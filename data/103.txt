In recent years, shopping has become a way of entertaining and is necessary for buying essentials. For a lot of people, shopping and going to the mall are errands, and they view it as draining. On the other hand, some people view shopping as a form of entertainment. They claim that shopping should be a way of taking a break from the world and responsibilities. This essay will compare the two sides and contrast them.

Looking first at people who view shopping as a way of entertainment. They claim that shopping should not be taken seriously, and people should enjoy it more. For example, when someone goes to the mall, they should enjoy it by going to a small café in the mall. While shopping, they could buy things they actually like and are genuinely interested in. By doing so, they will feel more relaxed by the end of it.

While on the other hand, people who claim that shopping is draining and only view it as an errand believe that shopping can never be a form of entertainment simply because, after shopping, someone will feel drained and exhausted. For example, when there is an important event coming up, that person will spend hours looking for the perfect outfit. As a result, that will cause them stress and exhaustion. So, they believe that shopping could never be a way of entertainment.

People who claim that shopping could never be a way of entertainment believe that shopping is only for getting essentials. While that could be true, it is all a matter of perspective and how someone views the things around them. Recent studies show that ninety-five percent of the people who view shopping as entertainment are happier than those who do not.

In conclusion, both viewing shopping as entertainment and not viewing it as such depends on the person. It is recommended that people all around the world should view shopping as a form of entertainment for their own well-being and mental health.
