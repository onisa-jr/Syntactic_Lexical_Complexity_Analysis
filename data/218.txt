Shopping nowadays is essential for our day-to-day needs, but some people use shopping as an excuse to entertain themselves and switch from their boring activities. Others, however, argue that shopping is just getting the stuff you need and then you are on with your day. I argue that shopping cannot be a way of entertainment, and I will explain why.

In our modern day and age, shopping can be a way to entertain ourselves. Instead of just going to the local shop or local store to speed things up, you can go to the mall where there is a supermarket that has various items you can choose from. You can find items that are interesting to try, which can be entertaining and might be worth trying again.

You also get to explore the various clothing brands that you cannot find in the local clothing shops, which is very exciting to check. Brands like H and M and American Eagle offer you high-quality clothes with such low prices.

Instead of spending the day only buying your groceries and clothes, you can try new things. For example, going to the library in the mall and reading a book—you might find something to your liking. Also, trying a restaurant can be a way of entertainment.

In conclusion, I find shopping is a way of getting what you need, but also it can be fun and provide new experiences that help you enjoy the day and not feel bored. Even though it is not entertainment, it gives you entertainment when you are doing it. So, shopping is not for fun, but it can be fun depending on how you look at it.
