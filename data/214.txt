In today's world, shopping is easier than before, especially with online shopping that has become very famous. Many people like to buy things even if they do not really need them, just because it makes them feel good or just to have something new. Although, some people think it is not a smart way to use money.

There are people who think shopping is a kind of relaxing activity. They go online and see what they want to buy. They do not even have to go outside, just sit in their bed and wait for the delivery to come to their door. Sometimes they buy things again that they already own, not because they forget, but because they like to look at them or they think maybe they will need them later.

Shopping can also be a hobby for some, especially when new things come out like shoes, electronics, or something popular. People want to be part of the trend and stay up to date with the latest things. It gives them a feeling of joy and excitement.

Other people believe that shopping is not important. They think it is a waste of money and time, maybe because they have different priorities, or they do not care about new things like clothes or gadgets. Or maybe they are just not into shopping at all.

To finish, each person has their own choice. If shopping makes you feel happy, then do it. Some people buy for fun, and others only when needed, so it depends on what you like.
