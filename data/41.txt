Shopping can be described as a girl's best activity. Shopping is not just about buying what is necessary anymore. Shopping is buying anything, whether it is a necessity or a luxury. 

Shopping for entertainment can be seen in two ways: either a shopaholic or a moderately reasonable shopper.

A shopaholic can be described as a person who is addicted to shopping. They often focus on shopping itself. It does not matter whether it is a necessity or a luxury, which proves that shopping can be for entertainment.

A moderately reasonable shopper still shops for fun but to a much lower level. They prioritize the essentials and yet still love spas for the fun part.

It is argued that shopping should be just for essentials and not for entertainment because it can lead to financial problems and could potentially lead to addiction.

However, that point is not important since people can still enjoy the luxury of shopping for fun while maintaining a budget according to their financial situation and avoiding the financial problem. 

As for the addiction, it is shown in studies that shopaholics are decreasing, and being considered one means shopping ten times in a week, which is above the average person, about ten times.

In conclusion, shopping for entertainment is normal. Lots of people do it, including shopaholics and moderate shoppers, which proves that yes, it can be for entertainment indeed. A great advice is prioritizing the necessity and creating a budget for the fun part of shopping.
