Some people have arguments about shopping for fun or shopping for needs. Shopping is considered a place to buy clothes or any items you need from time to time. Most people shop for entertainment. It is either to spend some time there, go out with some friends, or to take shopping as a workout by walking.

Shopping to spend some time is either out of boredom or just to see what is new in stores. Some people would go out shopping not with the intention of actually buying things.

One of the most common reasons is spending some time with their friends while looking at some clothing or trying out some of them for fun.

One of the reasons people go out for shopping is to walk a long distance. It is either because they feel unhappy, and taking a walk in the mall or any shopping place makes them feel better.

Many people might disagree, saying, "Why waste time on shopping if you are not going to buy something?" But it is not a waste of time if they feel comfortable just spending some time having fun. Also, some people find shopping a way to escape stress and daily problems. It becomes like a mini vacation.

In conclusion, shopping for entertainment is a good way to spend some time, go out with some friends, or take a walk. It is considered a way to entertain yourself. Therefore, shopping is not only for buying needs, but also for relaxing, seeing new things, and enjoying your time without any pressure or worries.
