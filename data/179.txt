Shopping is indeed necessary, but is it fun? Some people would say that shopping is exhausting and not fun. However, I disagree. Shopping could be entertaining and fun. In this essay, I would argue why shopping could be a fun activity to do.

Shopping could bring happiness to humans. Being able to get yourself what you love and what you like could decrease your stress and anxiety. Knowing your favorite kind of items would make your shopping easier. So, if you are doing something mentally draining, promise yourself that if you finish it, you will reward yourself with your favorite kind of clothing or items.

Next, shopping for gifts. Getting gifts for your family or friends will make you feel better about yourself, knowing you made someone you love happy. Along with them bringing you gifts, shopping for gifts is a fun activity to do with the people you love. You could make a wish list of the things you wish for, and people will shop for them for you. You can search for your friend's wish list and shop for their favorite items to make their day.

Along with gifts, there are the necessary items. If you go shopping and buy the necessary items, it will bring you relief knowing that you got yourself everything you need. You have to be in the mood for shopping. If you go shopping while stressed, you could forget a bunch of things you need or maybe get items you do not need by accident. Have fun and loosen up while shopping. It is your right to get yourself what you need and what you love.

Overall, shopping could decrease stress and bring you relief, feelings of happiness, and comfort. Rewarding yourself with shopping will benefit you when doing chores and homework, along with shopping for gifts for your family, cousins, and friends. It could be a great activity and entertaining for humans.
