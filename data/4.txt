Nowadays, shopping has become a form of entertainment and not just a way to buy what is needed. It is common today for most people to go shopping in their free time or to buy some unnecessary things. It is an enticing activity. However, some people believe that shopping is only for buying necessary things. They argue that it should not be for entertainment. This may be true, but shopping can actually be very exciting because it can be considered a social activity and can make people happy.

Shopping can be a way to socialize and be an activity. Going shopping is a way to communicate with others. Some people like to meet each other at malls and go shopping together. Moreover, many people go shopping as an activity or even as a hobby. Therefore, shopping could be a useful activity.

Another reason is that shopping can be used to make others happy. For example, it is used to buy presents and make somebody happy. Buying presents could be the best thing about shopping. In addition, shopping is a way to rest from work and tiring activities and to reward ourselves. So, shopping is a nice way to be glad and free of worries.

Some argue that shopping should not be for entertainment. They claim that it is a waste of time and money. However, this is not true because there are ways to shop moderately and avoid wasting. For instance, one can follow a routine for shopping and specify how much to spend. People can be wise when they shop for entertainment.

To conclude, shopping can make everyone happier and can be used as entertainment. Buying some presents and socializing with others are exciting things about shopping. Shopping could be the best way to entertain ourselves.
