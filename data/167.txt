Shopping is one of the things people do often in their lives. Some people go by themselves, and others go with someone or a group. Because of this, people are thinking about whether shopping is something you need or just something for fun. In this writing, I will talk about how shopping is not really fun but something we need to live.

It is true that shopping can feel good and fun. You can forget your stress and feel better afterward. However, those feelings mostly come from people who do not do the real shopping. They are usually young or live with family members who buy everything. That is why they think shopping is a fun activity. However, their thought is not very deep and is not based on real-life needs.

When we think about it more, shopping is clearly a need. It is not only about buying, but about choosing what is best for your life, like the food you eat or the clothes you wear to work or school. People who do not have a lot of money shop only for what they need, not just for joy. This shows that shopping is something humans need, not just a game or something to pass the time.

To finish, there are many ways people think about shopping. Some say it is fun, and some say it is important. All ideas are acceptable, but some make more sense, like how people need to buy things to survive. That makes shopping more serious and necessary in daily living.
