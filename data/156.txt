Why do we shop? In the past, shopping was only for getting what is necessary. However, with human development and money resources increasing, people started to shop more. In this essay, we will discuss why people shop for entertainment instead of just getting what is necessary.

Shopping is an activity where people take things from a store or a market and pay money for them. Nowadays, shopping for people has become a form of entertainment. Some women would go to the stores or markets and buy items. However, they might not need them. But it gives them relief and joy. Moreover, we think that this behavior could escalate and become an addiction. Sometimes, men also go with a friend and buy clothes or objects that are not needed. Also, people like to post what they buy on the internet, which makes others feel they also need to go shopping. This cycle continues and may never stop.

As we just mentioned, becoming excessive in any habit can lead to bad consequences, such as shopping when it is not needed. A person should control himself or herself, and should not follow every desire or moment of enjoyment. This is a really bad habit that can affect your life negatively. When someone cannot stop this behavior, it can cause money problems and stress as well. That is why it is important to think before buying.

To conclude, shopping has been the act of buying things from the beginning of time until now. Furthermore, some people use it to fulfill emotional needs. But it is important to be careful not to become addicted.
