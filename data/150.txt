These days, people have started buying things they do not even want just to make other people think they are rich or happy. This is not only harmful to them, but also to the society around them.

First of all, in the past, people used to appreciate what they had, like one jacket that lasted for years, and they did not think about changing their phone every year. Nowadays, people would rather buy a new phone just because they want the latest version, not because their phone is broken. A person who is saving money for their future is surely better than someone who is spending on things that will not last. So, you have to make the right decision before it is too late.

Second of all, shopping has become a way to forget problems instead of solving them-a quick fix that causes more problems. The first step should be knowing the difference between want and need. Try understanding yourself and why you feel the need to spend. Maybe help someone instead and feel the joy of giving.

Third of all, people think happiness comes from material things, while in reality, it is the complete opposite. True joy comes from love and experiences. Others may say they are investing in good things, but I think they are just trying to explain it.

Fourth of all, I cannot agree with what is happening. People are losing control and becoming slaves to brands and trends.

To conclude, shopping should be something useful, not harmful, and people need to wake up before it is too late.
