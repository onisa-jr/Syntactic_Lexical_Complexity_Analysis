Shopping is not only about buying things. Some people may say that shopping is just for getting what you need in daily life, but I believe that is not always true. Shopping is also a kind of fun for many people. In this essay, I will talk about why I agree with that idea.

First, shopping can make people feel better even if they do not buy something important. For example, some people like to walk in shops and look at new items. They feel relaxed and enjoy the view of new clothes, electronics, or books. Even when they are not buying, they still feel excited. Other people think shopping is boring. These people just go to buy what they want and leave quickly. They do not like the crowd, the noise, or the long lines. They believe it is a waste of time and money.

But I prefer to think shopping is more than that. Sometimes, we just want to go outside and change our mood. Going to shopping places can help us forget the stress. Even if we buy just a small thing, it can make us feel better. We could spend time with friends or family while shopping and also have fun.

In conclusion, shopping is not only about what we need. It is also about our feelings and how we enjoy our time. Life is not always about serious things. We should also do activities that make us happy, and shopping can be one of those activities.
