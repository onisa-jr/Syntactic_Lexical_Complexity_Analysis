For some people, shopping is a form of entertainment rather than for buying the necessary stuff. However, shopping for fun is bad. It is irresponsible, makes people buy useless stuff, causes them to lose a lot of money, and results in buying illegitimate things.

Although shopping for entertainment makes people happy, it is not responsible. They would buy things they do not need or even benefit from, like an electric eraser when they already have a good regular one.

Moreover, shopping as a form of entertainment could let people go out with their friends and have fun. However, when they do that a lot, they end up buying too much, which causes them to lose money.

Moving forward, when people shop just to have a good time, they will be entertained. Even though, they could end up getting scammed and buying bad products. That is going to make them feel bad.

Also, when they go to the mall every day just to hang out, they buy snacks or shirts they do not want. That is not good and not smart.

Some people even borrow money or use credit cards just for shopping fun, which brings big debt later.

In addition, they might buy things only because they are on sale, and not because they really need them.

So that is why shopping for fun is not always a good idea and can be harmful in many ways.

In conclusion, even though some think that shopping for entertainment is better than buying only what is necessary, others think the opposite, because it causes them to buy useless things, spend a lot of money, and even get scammed by buying fake things.
