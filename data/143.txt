Shopping is the process where a customer buys things and pays for them. Things can be items such as bags or clothes. Such things bring a lot of joy to people because they fulfill their needs, whether emotional needs, like bringing them joy when they feel down, or boosting their confidence when they feel low. It can also be a process where they buy only what is necessary, with no emotion involved.

For me, I am in between. Sometimes I will shop for entertainment, and sometimes I will get only what is necessary. This essay will discuss why people have such ideas about shopping.

People who think of shopping as only a way to get what is necessary are more specific and mindful. They might be the type who writes a list before buying what they need, and the candy shelf will not attract their attention. They might also be good at saving money and have better control over themselves in a world full of goods. They are casual people who wear black, white, and grey.

However, people who claim that shopping is a form of entertainment are more relaxed and free. They might have less control over themselves because of how much money they can spend on any desire they have, without minding any responsibilities. They are well-dressed.

In the end, shopping can reveal a lot about how a person handles their life in a way that makes them feel comfortable. For me, there is no right form of shopping, but it surely tells a lot about people—how they think, how they live, and how they look.
