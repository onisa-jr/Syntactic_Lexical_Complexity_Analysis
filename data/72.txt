For some people, they view shopping for necessary things such as food, clothing, and house items, and not just for entertainment. For some, shopping can be an activity and a source of happiness, and it does not have to be a priority. Although I disagree that it has to be only for necessary things.

Firstly, shopping does not always have to depend on the mood of the person. Sometimes it can be done simply because it is something they feel like doing at a random moment during the day. Some people shop when they feel bored, and others when they want to celebrate, which is not always out of need. Shopping sometimes provides stress relief and enjoyment when nothing else does.

Secondly, going to malls or markets is not just for buying, but also for walking and seeing what new things are on the shelves, even if they do not buy them. People get inspiration and ideas from window shopping and seeing what others are buying, wearing, or using.

Thirdly, many people buy things online just because they look good or because the items are on sale, not because they need them. This kind of shopping is not necessary, but it is still important to them and is not always a bad thing.

In my opinion, shopping is not only for necessary things but also an emotional and social experience. People bond when shopping, talk about what they like, and share ideas. Even if it is not for food or clothes, it still matters to them.

Therefore, I think shopping is not only for necessity but also for many other reasons that are valid.
