For some people, shopping is not about buying what is necessary. It forms a significant role in entertainment. The journey of shopping can be joyful and relaxing.

However, some people find happiness in shopping, whether in malls or via online platforms, and some people use it as an escape from daily pressures. In shopping, you can find unique items or interesting things that make you happy.

Enjoyment in shopping can occur while you are buying your needs, connecting with your friends, or going shopping with them to collect special items. Seeing other people can also be enjoyable for some. Visiting different places each time creates a different experience.

Nowadays, malls have become places that offer almost everything: clothes, home needs, food, and much more. Some places also include entertainment centers for kids. Many people also spend time shopping just to walk inside and look at the offers, even if they have no plan to buy anything. For some, spending time around shops improves their mood. The lights and colors give them happy feelings. Some go shopping because they feel lonely or want a change of scenery, and this helps them feel better even if they do not buy anything. Online shopping also makes people feel excited when they wait for delivery, and opening the package is like a small celebration.

In conclusion, shopping has become more than just fulfilling your needs. However, some people do not feel any joy while shopping. People can shop, eat, and play in the same place. That is why it is a form of entertainment.
