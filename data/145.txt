Shopping has existed since a long time, and people back then in the fifteenth century shopped for necessary needs. But later, some people started to go shopping for fun. Some people do not see that it is entertainment.

Some families take their kids to the mall and all shop together as quality time to have fun by buying clothes, home needs, or furniture. They are going to have lots of fun, or some people like to shop alone.

On the other hand, there are a lot of people who hate going to the mall. They think it is just for necessary things, not for fun. They also think it is a noisy place, and it is not made for having fun.

Shopping is a necessary thing to do, and my belief is that shopping is not fun. You can go anywhere else to have fun, but not stand and wait in a long line for the cashier. People are different. Not everyone will agree with me, but there are some people like me.

Entertainment is a concept that differs from one person to another. We all know that we are not the same, and we all have different ways to have fun and ways to get the necessary needs.

In conclusion, shopping is like a duty for some and like a play place for others. But with this modern life, there is more than the malls. That does not mean we should judge. We all have our own perspective on shopping, and we all have our own way to shop.
