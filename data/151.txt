Sometimes, when you are upset and you want to cheer up yourself, you can go out for a walk or a ride in your car. However, for some people, they immediately go shopping, which is one of the ways that could change their mood.

For me, I do agree that shopping is an amazing way to change your mood, but not without company. So yes, I do enjoy buying new stuff either from the mall or even online. I do see shopping as an effective way to cheer yourself, but also it sometimes becomes a waste of money when you just keep buying things that you do not need.

Shopping is one of the best ways to have fun and change your mood, but it is not the only way. You can always go for a walk or a ride in the car. For me, I do enjoy other people's company while shopping. So to me, mostly I enjoy the presence of my friends, sisters, and family more than the shopping itself.

For others who prefer to spend some alone time, shopping is a magical way to kill time and relax by shopping alone at the mall.

In conclusion, I do agree that shopping can also be for fun, not just about your needs. However, it should have a limit. We cannot always shop for fun, so we do not end up buying things we do not need. Also, we can enjoy ourselves with many things other than shopping.
