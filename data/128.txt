Shopping is necessary for all of us, whether for important things or not. We do not live without buying anything, even things that are not important, because it is human nature. In this essay, it will show what I believe and whether it is only necessary to buy important things or not.

I believe that it is really important to buy things even though we do not need them and we just want them. If we do not have extra money, we would buy only necessary things. If we do have extra money, we would entertain ourselves. We should buy things we need, not only necessary things, because only having the necessary things would not be enough for our life.

I disagree that we should only buy necessary things. If I wanted something, I would not buy it for myself just because it is not necessary. I would not have passion for my life. It would not be a good life and I would not enjoy my life anymore if we only kept ourselves to necessary things.

However, life is simple and easy. We should live our life and not complain about what God gave us. We should be thankful and accept whatever I have. Buying only necessary things would still include a lot of things that human beings need. We could not spend too much money, but we can arrange it.

To sum up, money is needed in our life. If we have more than enough, we would buy anything and entertain ourselves. If we do not, we would accept having less and buy only the necessary things. Having a lot of money could not buy happiness, but it can help arrange it in some way. Buy something we love. Buy something that makes us happy. It might be more enjoyable rather than having no money.

It might not be living my best life if I do not buy anything I want and only buy necessary things. Life does not work like that. It feels like a useless life without any entertainment. Life would be easier if we enjoy our life and spend not just wisely, but also for what we feel like.
