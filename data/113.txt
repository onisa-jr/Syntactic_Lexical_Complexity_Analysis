Some people find shopping as a therapy. On the other hand, some people find it unnecessary. Some think it is a waste of time, while others enjoy shopping and find it necessary. However, people disagree, saying it is a waste of money.

Wasting your time on something you like could raise your happiness. According to Harvard research in human studies, it is found that this raises adrenaline and makes people happy for simply wasting time on what they like. Although people enjoy just looking at dresses and new fashion clothes, they enjoy shopping and spending money on it.

Some people say that shopping is necessary. Wearing new clothes is good for your appearance to the people who see you every day and gives them a good opinion about you. This is according to multiple experiences of people who find shopping necessary to them.

On the other hand, a large number of people disagree with all these reasons, saying that it is a waste of money. If you have money, spend it on something that will benefit you in your future life. Think about the necessary things in life. Do not just go along with the trend to wear new clothes and dresses every day.

In my opinion, I think if something will make you happy and will not affect you negatively, then do what you like. If spending this amount of money will increase your confidence in yourself, then go for it.

In summary, there are many opinions. Some think it is a waste of time, some enjoy shopping, and some find it necessary. On the other hand, others find it a waste of money. But in the end, do what makes you happy and comfortable.
