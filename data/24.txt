These past couple of years, shopping has become a form of entertainment more than a means of buying your necessary needs. Therefore, a new kind of addiction has formed.

First of all, people in the past used to buy their essential needs such as food, clothes, or even real estate so their children could benefit from it. Nowadays, people prefer to buy a new pair of shoes rather than save up for the future. A thirty-year-old who owns real estate is absolutely better off than a thirty-year-old with ten different pairs of designer shoes. So, you have to be careful about what you are getting yourself into.

Second of all, shopping can fill the void that those people feel, so they cannot stop. They feel better every time they do it. So, the first logical move is to stop for a while or at least make yourself stop. Try looking around to realize that life is not about money. Try to donate the money instead to people in need.

Third of all, life is more than money or how many designer bags you have. Some people may argue about the fabric and say that they are buying quality rather than the logo itself. I would argue and say that quality can be found everywhere.

Fourth of all, I would say that I strongly disagree with everything going on, whether it is the large amount of money spent every day or the amount of clothes these people have in their drawers.

To conclude, if you think that shopping is a form of entertainment, you should get help. Shopping is about buying necessary needs and maybe treating yourself once in a while. Any kind of addiction is horrible. You should be careful.
