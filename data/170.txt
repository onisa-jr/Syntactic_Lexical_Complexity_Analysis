Shopping can be a form of entertainment. For some people, shopping can be fun, other than the actual shop or purchase. Ever since malls were a thing, people usually spent time there. In this essay, I will explain it.

The process of shopping can be in two ways, either purchasing what is needed or hanging out there. Some people like buying new items every time they shop. Also, other people like looking or seeing what is new or popular at the moment. It is definitely an exciting activity for many people. For example, malls provide shows or food. Some people go there even if they do not need to buy something, just to walk or see people. Others may go with friends just because they want to hang out.

On the other hand, some people can have a different opinion about shopping in general. They see it as just buying what you need. It is seen as not fun, which is realistic in a way. They see it as a waste of time and would never enjoy actually hanging out. It can be boring to them. For example, they may have no time or not enjoy anything. It depends on the person.

In my opinion, I would agree that it can be a form of entertainment. While you shop, you can enjoy yourself a little here and there. There can also be something you might like while shopping. Sometimes shopping is not about money but about having a good time looking and talking.
