Many people see shopping as entertainment, while others believe it is only useful when needed. Although I respect those opinions, I personally do not agree with the idea of shopping for fun.

There are people who enjoy shopping to feel better or pass time. It can be like a small vacation from their real-life problems. Some say when they feel sad or bored, going to buy something makes them feel good and helps them forget their worries. They say it is a good way to relax and enjoy. Shopping can be a way to escape for them, especially when life is stressful or not happy.

But I think shopping should be more about purpose than pleasure. People usually go to the store to get important things like food, medicine, school supplies, or household items. It is not always about joy or happiness but more about responsibility. Shopping without purpose can be dangerous to someone's budget and future.

Also, shopping for fun too much can cause trouble. Spending money on things not needed can create financial problems. People may forget to save or pay bills. The time spent in shops or online can be used better for study, work, or family. Shopping should be done wisely, not just for fun or escape.

In conclusion, while some people enjoy shopping as a hobby, I believe it is mostly an activity to get what is needed. It should not be done only for fun because it can lead to regret or waste. Practical shopping is better than emotional shopping always.
