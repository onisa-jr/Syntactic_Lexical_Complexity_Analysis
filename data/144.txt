I agree that shopping can be an entertainment. You can agree with that or not, but for a lot of people, it is the idea that you go out, and it makes you feel less depressed. How about shopping?

When you feel low or bad, every person does whatever they want. Some take showers, some do exercises, and others will cook. Shopping as well.

The idea that shopping is only for buying whatever you need for the house or food or other necessary things is not always true. In our life, there are times when we want to recharge ourselves by shopping, buying clothes or games. Sometimes it can be jewelry or food. It can be expensive or cheap, it does not matter. For me, when I feel bad and low, or when I get a good mark, I go to buy what my soul needs, and a lot of the time it is food.

For those people who do not agree, I think the reason is because they did not find the enjoyment in spending money. They see that there are other necessities to spend money on. They may do something else for entertainment.

Finding what you love is great. Shopping is a way to express what you feel. Having a walk while you are shopping can heal your body without you knowing. Also, shopping can help you stop thinking about things that are not too important in your environment or in your life.

Sometimes you find things while you are shopping that will keep you busy when you are disappointed in life. In the end, I would like to say: do whatever you want. Do the thing in which you will find your entertainment.
