Online shopping carts are abandoned 80 percent of the time, which shows that shopping is not only for important needs but also for fun. Shopping can be a big source of entertainment in different ways.

Shopping is considered a way of wasting time and having fun for many people. Many people go shopping in the mall and online only as a way of wasting time, or consider it to be leisure time that reduces anxiousness and helps them relax.

Shopping also can be a way to express oneself. Self-expression can be through clothes, beauty products, and many other things. It is a way of having fun and discovering oneself more.

At last, shopping is also considered to be a personal reward. People usually go shopping in the mall or shop online as a way of rewarding themselves after accomplishing a goal or something else. This makes shopping a way of having fun.

Some people will argue and claim that shopping is only a way of getting their needs. It might be true, but not most of the time. A recent survey in the United States shows that sixty percent of people shopping online do not buy their important needs.

In conclusion, shopping in its different and various forms is considered to be a big source of fun in many different ways. Shopping can be a way of spending leisure time, expressing oneself, and rewarding oneself. However, people should be responsible and not spend too much on items they do not need.
