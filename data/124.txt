Shopping is something we all do in different ways. Some people shop only for needs, but others shop for fun. In my opinion, I believe shopping is not only for needs but also for wants. Sometimes, buying something you want is more joyful than buying what you need.

When I think about what people need, it is similar to surviving. They need food, water, clothes, and maybe a house. But is that everything? I do not think so. There are many things that people do not think are necessary, but for me, they are. For example, when I see a nice bag or beautiful shoes, I feel I want to get them. Maybe I do not need them, but I still buy them. Why? Because they make me smile.

Also, when I feel sad or have had a bad day, I go shopping. I walk around the store and see things. Sometimes I do not plan to buy anything, but I do. It is like therapy. Some people do not understand that. They think it is a waste. But I think it is not.

Some people only buy what they need and never spend more. They say it is smart. Maybe it is smart, but is it fun? I am not sure. For me, life is short, and sometimes we need to enjoy it.

In conclusion, shopping is not always about what you must have. Sometimes it is about what you like. Everyone has their own way of thinking. But for me, shopping for wants is just as important as shopping for needs.
