Shopping is an act of buying, an act of seeing different materials, or an act of entertainment. People agree and disagree with this statement. In this essay, I am agreeing that shopping is not just about buying what is necessary, but also a form of entertainment.

For different people, shopping is a form of entertainment. If you feel bad, you can go and shop for different items just to set up your mood. In other words, shopping is a physical act. From another point of view, a person may disagree.

Other people think that they should balance between shopping for entertainment and shopping for necessary things. For example, if you have only enough money to buy necessary things, do not spend it on other unnecessary things. You should be able to think about which one is more important: having fun or not. You can combine the two things by going to your favorite shop and buying a necessary item while enjoying the experience, for example. This is unlike some people who think that buying necessary things is not a fun activity.

Some people do not have the ability to buy things for entertainment. They may have little money, lack transportation, or believe that money should be spent on necessary things like studying, learning something new, or giving it to poor people who are really in need of it.

In conclusion, people are different, and each of them has their own thoughts about what to do with their money. However, I am a person who thinks, if it will make me happy, why not? What about you? Do you think that shopping is not just about buying what is necessary?
