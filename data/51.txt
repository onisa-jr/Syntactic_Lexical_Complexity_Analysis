Everyone loves shopping for no reason, especially for clothes, either from the mall or online. Moreover, for most people, shopping is their one and only comfort time. People buy things that are not necessary. It is a waste of money and time, and it can lead a person to become a shopaholic.

Shopping for entertainment is fun and expensive. Although it gives the person a feeling of excitement, it does not last long. In fact, for most people, when they realize how much money they spent, they immediately hate the main reason that made them lose money, which is the clothes they bought. As a result, they hate the clothes and never wear them.

People already know that shopping is a waste of money. However, they do not realize how much time they waste on shopping. If they only saw that there are a lot of things they can do other than shopping—like trying new things, being social, and developing their personality—they would not shop again, in order to save that time.

Most people believe that shopping for entertainment will not cause harm. However, it is the main and only reason for becoming a shopaholic. Being a shopaholic will not only affect the person themselves, which is wrong, but it will also affect their whole social circle.

Sometimes people shop when they feel bored and think that buying things will make them feel better, but they feel the same afterward.

Also, sometimes they forget what they already have and buy new things that are the same.

In conclusion, people keep shopping without thinking about why they do it and continue the cycle of wasting.
