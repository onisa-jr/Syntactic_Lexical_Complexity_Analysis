We can find entertainment in many different things. Some people would find it odd if anyone told them that they find shopping to be a form of entertainment. They believe shopping is a necessity. I disagree with that for many reasons that will be discussed in this essay.

Firstly, there is a lot of silly and unnecessary stuff that we find while shopping. That explains why shopping is not only for necessary things. The fact is, shopping for whatever makes me happy can also be considered a necessity.

Secondly, if shopping were only for necessary things, there would be no trendy clothes, accessories, and many more items. Trendy stuff can be unimportant or even ridiculous, but it is, in fact, something that brings joy to many of us.

On the other hand, many people would say that there are priorities, and trendy items or silly things bought for fun are not among their priorities. That might be true, but you do not have to be strict about what you are buying all the time. As a matter of fact, you can walk around the store and still not buy anything—just looking around for fun.

In conclusion, it is quite common that shopping helps relieve stress and helps people find joy and calmness. However, a lot of people disagree, saying it is a waste of time and money. I wish they could find entertainment in different things. Shopping is not for everyone, but for some people, it is more than just buying stuff.
