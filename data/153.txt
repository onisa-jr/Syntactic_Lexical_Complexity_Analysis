Shopping is an important thing in our daily life. Shopping can be considered an everyday task. Some people might go shopping three to four times a day. This essay will argue whether shopping is just to buy necessary items or just for entertainment.

Shopping is an important thing in someone's life. Shopping is to buy necessary items. However, that does not mean it is not for entertainment. For instance, when you shop online after receiving some money, later you will realize that you wanted something not because you needed it, but because you only wanted it. Another example is buying trendy clothing, bags, and makeup. These things are not necessary, yet we still buy them. Shopping is for both entertainment and for buying necessary things.

Other people might differ in opinion and disagree. Some people think that shopping is only for necessary things. For example, old people are still convinced that shopping is only for buying necessary items. The reason why they would think that way is because they believe that earning money is not easy. Another example is that some people have a low salary or do not have a job to earn money. Due to these reasons, people still think that shopping is only for buying important things such as food, phones, and normal clothing. These situations might cause them to think that way.

To sum up, people have different reasons, beliefs, lifestyles, or different situations. In the end, whether it is entertainment for some people or not, shopping is an important thing in our life. I believe shopping can be entertainment.
