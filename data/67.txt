Shopping is fun. People often shop a lot online or in actual stores. For some people, shopping is a type of habit. They buy unnecessary stuff. Do you agree that shopping should not be just buying what you need, but a form of entertainment?

Shopping should not be considered a form of entertainment because shopping is a waste of money. Buying clothes, shoes, and makeup you do not need, just for fun, would consume your money. Not only for an unemployed person, but even for people with jobs, it will lead to a closet full of items you do not need or have never looked at.

Taking shopping as a habit may lead to addiction. People with a shopping addiction often started by treating shopping as a habit, which led them to this situation.

People who see shopping as a form of entertainment argue that shopping could be a habit. It is fun and mentally refreshing, just like reading and swimming. However, if reading or any other habit is harming you, you must stop it. Strong studies have shown that 80 percent of people who shop for fun develop shopping addiction.

To sum up, shopping is joyful as long as it does not cross the limit, waste your money, or harm your well-being. After reading this essay, you should be careful with your wallet and your mental health.
