Shopping can be fun on some days. It is not necessary to buy what you need, but you can also buy things that you will never use just because you like them. There are a lot of apps that allow us to shop from home, like SHEIN, Namshi, and so on. We have the choice if we want to shop from the store or just buy whatever we want while we are sitting in our home. It is our way to explain our feelings.

First, online shopping went viral because of how easy and fast it is. People started to use online shopping to save time and energy. This is not the only reason. In my opinion, it is very comfortable to choose what you like when you are just sitting on your comfortable bed. However, some people still enjoy the traditional way, which is shopping in stores.

Second, shopping may not always be about needing something or liking it. Sometimes, we shop because we feel sad or angry. So, it is more like expressing our feelings through going to the store to get both fresh air and new clothes, or just lying in bed and scrolling through the online store to get whatever we want or need.

Some people say that shopping should only be done when needed. But as I said, it is not necessary to buy only what you need. It would not be fun to shop that way. Shopping is a relaxing moment where you take whatever makes you happy, not just what keeps your house organized. Shopping is more like a way to express our feelings, our needs, and what we love to have or give as gifts to our loved ones. Sometimes we buy what other people need, not what we need.

In conclusion, people can just have what they want as long as they have the ability to pay for it, not only if they have the need for it. If spending money can make us happy, then why do we not just spend it, even if we are not going to use what we buy?
