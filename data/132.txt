Are you shopping to buy or to have fun? These days, everyone is shopping online or going to a store. People think shopping is only for buying necessary items like food. However, sometimes we buy food just to have fun during activities such as watching TV, for example. In this essay, we will discuss shopping for entertainment.

In previous years, people thought and believed that shopping was only for buying things they needed. For example, they did not have different types of stores or online shops. Nowadays, we have a lot of stores through which we can buy small things such as anime figures just to entertain ourselves. There are also people who go to stores just to walk and look around without the need to buy anything. This can also be fun and help the mind feel relaxed. A lot of people take pictures and post them on social media.

Another reason is that going shopping and buying things changes our mood and makes us happier. For this reason, which is important, a lot of people disagree with those who say shopping must be only for necessary things. Making yourself happy is necessary.

On the other hand, people who do not agree with shopping for fun think that it is a waste of time and money, which is correct. However, if they think about the benefits of shopping for fun, they may change their minds.

To sum up, people have their reasons for buying for entertainment and wanting to have fun. On the other hand, some do not agree with them. It depends on you and how you see shopping.
