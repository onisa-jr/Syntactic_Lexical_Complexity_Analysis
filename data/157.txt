Shopping makes everyone happy, whether it is buying what is necessary or a form of entertainment. Everyone goes shopping just to change their mood. This essay will state why shopping is not just about buying what is necessary, but also a form of entertainment.

When it comes to mentioning the word "shopping," everyone gets excited, not because it is something necessary to do, but because it is entertaining and fun. It can also be a way to change the mood if you are in a bad one. In addition, it is beneficial for the eyes because we need to explore and observe in order to benefit our knowledge if needed.

As for mentioning that it is a way to escape reality, some people argue about this point. They view shopping as a waste of money, and they believe it is not entertaining when you spend too much due to multiple visits to the mall or other places. However, in this context, if you divide your money monthly, it could really help in avoiding wasteful spending.

In conclusion, each one of us deserves to entertain themselves. You do not need to think about the negative aspects of everything. If the subject is your happiness and mental health, then just enjoy and live. Shopping is one of the factors that influence others. If you see someone going to the mall, you would want to go too. Lastly, shopping is for entertainment and enjoyment, not only for necessity. Just enjoy your life to the fullest.
