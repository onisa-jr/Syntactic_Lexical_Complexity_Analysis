Shopping is something that many people do all the time. It can be something fun, and it can also be something people have to do. It goes back to the person and how they see it for themselves.

Shopping is, for sure, something fun. This shows in different ways. For instance, you have been saving money for a while, and now you want to treat yourself to a pair of shoes you have always wanted. Or maybe you are feeling a little down, and you want to go to the mall and look around at things to feel better. Another way is that maybe you just finished a big task and want to get yourself something as a prize, like a new phone case or a jacket you saw online. So yes, shopping is not always because you need something. It can be just because you want to feel good. Even buying a gift for a friend can be fun shopping too.

On the other side, shopping is not always for fun. It is also something needed in life. For example, when you have no food in the house, you have to go shopping for groceries. Or when your shoes are broken, and you need a new pair for work. Another example is when your shampoo is finished. You have to buy more because it is part of hygiene. These are times when shopping is a must, not only a want.

To finish, shopping can be entertainment or necessary. It really depends on the person and the reason, and sometimes it is both at the same time.
