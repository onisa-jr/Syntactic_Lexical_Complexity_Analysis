Some people might think that shopping is just for fun, something they can do in their free time, not because they have to.

Shopping brings joy to some people, and it might bring them happiness and calmness. Shopping involves many things, like shopping for food, cars, clothes, personal needs, and more. Some people shop regularly, and some have a schedule they have dedicated specifically for shopping. Also, sometimes people shop because they are bored and have nothing to do at that moment. Some people walk around the mall for hours even if they do not buy anything, but that is still considered shopping.

Although some people might think that shopping is just for entertainment, that is not correct. Shopping is important for buying clothes to look presentable in front of coworkers and classmates. Plus, studies show that shopping can change a person's mood. Shopping can be done alone or with friends, and it depends on the mood of the person doing it. Even online shopping is becoming more common because of technology, and people like to use their phones.

However, a lot of people disagree and think shopping is a waste of money and time, but that is not necessarily true because you can still shop while being responsible and not wasting money or time.

In conclusion, shopping is not just for entertainment. It is an important thing to do in order to fulfill personal needs. Therefore, it is not just a waste of money and time.
