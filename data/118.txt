In our daily life, we are doing many things: things that we need and things that make us happy. Did you ever stop and think, is it possible to enjoy yourself while doing what is needed? This essay talks about shopping. Is it only for needs, or is it also something to enjoy?

First and most important, doing something useful while being happy is the best way to live. For example, going shopping with your friend or with your brother or sister. You will buy what you need and, at the same time, have fun. It is like two birds with one stone: you get both. Another example is that you will not feel the time because you are busy and talking. That way, you do not feel tired or bored. Also, walking in a mall with someone you like will make you smile more. You feel more comfortable.

But some people do not see shopping like that. They think shopping is only about buying what is missing. They go in, take what they want, and leave. No talking, no laughing—just finish and go. They say it is a waste of time to walk around or look at other things.

Also, I understand them. Not everyone wants to spend extra money or spend a long time. Some people just want to go quickly and not think much. If you go alone, sometimes you buy less.

To end this, try going with someone next time. It might change how you feel. Maybe you will buy what you need and also have a good time.
