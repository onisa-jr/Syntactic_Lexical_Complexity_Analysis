There are many reasons why people like going to the shopping mall, and not just for buying things. It is well known that the mall is full of shops, but it also has places to relax and spend a good time. Some people even say that they go to the mall just for walking around or passing time. This essay is discussing why the mall is not only a place for shopping, but also for enjoying.

First reason, people enjoy walking around inside the mall. The mall is always clean and cold, so it is a good place to walk even when it is hot outside. Also, some malls have music playing, which makes the walking more pleasant. Even though some people say it is a waste of time, many people think it is not.

Second reason, children can have fun in the mall. There are many games and activities for them. Parents can leave their kids playing and go shopping without worry. While some people think the mall is not safe, it is usually very secure. The lights and sounds make it exciting for kids to play and enjoy games.

Third reason, people go to the mall to take a break from daily life. Sitting in a coffee shop or eating ice cream makes them feel relaxed. It is better than staying at home all day. Other people do not agree with this, but it is true for many. The mall is a good place to forget stress.

In conclusion, the mall is not just for buying, but also for having fun. People walk, enjoy games, and eat in the mall. It is a place that gives happiness and not only for shopping.
