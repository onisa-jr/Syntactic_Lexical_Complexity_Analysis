Shopping is for happiness also, and when you go shopping, it can be for no reason. Sometimes I just go to shop without thinking, because thinking is not always needed when you want to have fun. Shopping is like a game, and sometimes I win. Shopping is not only serious.

Shopping is, for many people, just something to do when they need things. But I do not think that way, because for me, shopping is more than buying something important. When I am bored, or tired, or even on just a normal day, I go to the store and see what I like. Maybe I do not need it, but I want it, and that is reason enough to buy it. One day, I saw a toy, and I bought it. Then I laughed and felt better. That is shopping for me.

People who do not shop for fun are missing something. Maybe they want to save money, or maybe they do not like malls, but still, I think they should try it. Shopping can be done from home too. You click and buy and feel happy. It is not hard, and it makes you smile.

In conclusion, shopping is a good way to feel better and to enjoy time. If you feel sad, or tired, or not happy, just go and shop for something. You do not need to think very hard. Shopping is easy, fast, and fun. I like shopping online more because I do not like to walk too much. Shopping always makes me feel good.
