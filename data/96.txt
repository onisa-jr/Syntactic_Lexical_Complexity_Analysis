Some people say that shopping is not just about buying what is necessary, but a form of entertainment. In my opinion, shopping is for necessary things and also an entertainment activity. Some people agree, and some do not. Buying things is like a hobby for some people. They enjoy shopping online or even in the mall. In this essay, I will argue about shopping — is it just for necessity, or also for entertainment?

Some people think shopping is just for important necessary things, like groceries or necessary clothing. These people believe they do not need to shop often. They see shopping as a necessary task. They believe that shopping is a waste of time. On the other hand, some people think that shopping is entertainment, and they have fun doing it. For those people, shopping is necessary and essential in their life, not like those who see shopping as a waste of time. The people who see shopping as entertainment are more likely to spend more money on their shopping. For some people, shopping is a hobby. They love doing it even if it is not for themselves — they like shopping for others. The people who have a passion for shopping and love it are mostly women, whether it is online or at the mall. For men, shopping is not that necessary, except for essential important things.

In my opinion, I agree that shopping is entertainment for necessary things. I enjoy buying things important to me or to my house. It is a responsible thing to do, and I feel happy and enjoy doing it.

In conclusion, shopping is an important thing in our life. Some think it is fun and entertaining, and some believe it is a waste of time. Different people believe in different things. Not enjoying shopping is a common thing among men and older people, and enjoying shopping is mostly common among women.
