Shopping is such an entertainment, whether it is online or not. I agree that it is an entertainment because it is a kind of reward for you after a long time of hard work.

Shopping throughout the year has been known as getting your tasks done and fulfilling what the house needs, but it is more than that. Sometimes it is a treat for yourself, especially when the payment comes. Yes, sometimes the house needs items, et cetera, but also, buying for your house or your room is entertainment. So either way, pick a side—it is called entertainment.

Let us continue. Take it from another angle. When you are buying a gift for your friend, relative, or even your siblings, it is a happy experience because you are picturing their surprise and their joyful tears about your gift to them, and them thanking you and loving you. It is an entertainment.

However, other people have different opinions. They say it is a kind of addiction or a waste of money. They say to save your money for when you really need it. Maybe I agree with them, but I have saved my money for necessary things before, and I still started to buy groceries as if it was important. So either way, I spend the money. "Addiction" is a big word for buying things you love for yourself or for anyone else, so no—it is not an addiction. It is an entertainment.

In conclusion, I agree with the statement, "For some people, shopping is not just about buying what is necessary, but a form of entertainment." Yes, I stated it again just for confirmation. Do not take life so seriously. Yes, there are things that are necessary, but do not stop rewarding yourself for every achievement—or even for no achievement. So yes, shopping is an entertainment.
