As the world grapples with the consequences of online shopping, the need and demand have increased widely. This new method of shopping became a trend in 2019, when 65 percent of people considered it a dependable method of shopping. Although most people find online shopping important, they should be more aware of cyber theft and overpaid taxes.

First and foremost, cyber theft is an important aspect that one should care about. According to the study conducted by Jame Arthur (2022), 45 percent of people experience fraud every year. While it is entertaining to shop online, one must be well educated and aware of cyber theft and how it operates. This reason is essential, if not the most highly recommended, by the authorities.

In addition to cyber theft, taxes and shipping fees are not seen as a form of entertainment. According to the Ministry of Commerce (MOC), 70 percent of people spend half of their salary on taxes, aside from the normally paid value-added tax. This demonstrates a downfall in people’s financial systems and needs to be observed.

Some may argue that online shopping can be seen as a form of entertainment regardless of any consequences. However, cyber theft and overpaid taxes are vital aspects to look upon. Many people are affected either financially or mentally. According to the study conducted by Harbor Tim (2023), the number of cases affected by cyber theft is increasing every day, which makes this aspect highly important due to the danger that needs to be considered.

In conclusion, while online shopping can be seen as a form of entertainment, people’s safety is far more important. With the increase in cases of cyber theft and overpaid taxes, it is vital for a sustainable community to pay attention to these recommendations and use online shopping wisely.
