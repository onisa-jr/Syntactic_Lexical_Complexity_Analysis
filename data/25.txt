Shopping is, without a doubt, a form of entertainment, at least for some people. While it is true that shopping is mostly done for buying necessities, many people like to shop just for the fun of it.

Some people would argue that shopping without a real purpose is a waste of time. However, that would quickly be proven wrong by simply stating a fact: shopping for entertainment is also a form of bonding if done with others, like your loved ones.

Some would also say there is no benefit in shopping for fun. In reality, it can be perfect for boosting someone’s mood. It is a simple pleasure in life, and that is all the reason it needs for it to be considered a benefit.

People should not be ridiculed if they shop for entertainment. There are times when people go to shopping malls not to buy anything, but just to walk around and look at things. That is completely valid because it gives a sense of comfort that is hard to explain. Even when the weather is bad or someone is feeling low, just going to the mall and looking at things can be enough to feel a bit better, which is something that is important.

In conclusion, the idea that shopping always has a reason is too strict, and not everyone fits into that box. That should be accepted more.

All in all, shopping is in fact a form of fun for some people, and I, for one, agree with the statement.
