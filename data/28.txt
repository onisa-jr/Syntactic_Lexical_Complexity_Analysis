Shopping is an important thing in our life. Many people go shopping every day. Some people enjoy shopping even if they do not buy anything. This essay will discuss if shopping is only to buy needed items or just for entertainment.

Shopping is an important thing for many people. Shopping is to buy important things, but it can be not only that. For example, sometimes people go shopping when they feel bored. Also, when they get money, they buy something they want, not because they need it. Another example is when someone buys clothes or perfumes even though they already have many at home. Some people buy makeup or accessories just because it looks nice. So, shopping is not only for necessary items but also for fun. People like to go to the shopping mall and look around at different things. Even if they do not buy, it feels nice.

Some people think differently about this. They believe shopping is only for things we must have. For example, old people think shopping is for food and medicine. They do not agree with shopping just for fun. Also, people who have financial problems cannot spend money on things they do not need. They only buy important things like rice, clothes, or a phone card. This makes them think shopping is not for enjoyment but only for survival. That is why they do not like extra shopping. For them, shopping is a serious thing.

In conclusion, people have different thoughts and lifestyles. Some go shopping for enjoyment, and some only for what they need. I believe that shopping is not only for buying necessities but also for fun sometimes. Shopping is still an important thing in our life.
