Do you consider shopping as entertainment? Many people in the city of Riyadh consider shopping as something fun to do. However, in other cities, people only go shopping when it is necessary. In this essay, I would like to point out why I see shopping as entertainment.

In the city of Riyadh, many people, including myself, find it hard to do anything other than shop due to the hot temperature and the lack of activities. Shopping is the only thing you can do in such circumstances. Shopping allows you to try new things and see new things. Keep in mind that shopping is not only for fun; it could help people interact with each other.

However, many people say that shopping for entertainment is a waste of time and money. Although this may be true, people who have self-control can shop without worrying about such things. To prevent yourself from wasting money, you could create a budget for yourself and still have fun.

I read a statement before that said buying the things you love will not make you poor; it will make you happy. So, for those who go shopping for fun, they are not doing anything wrong. People who find it hard to meet others can go shopping and meet people who have similar tastes to them.

Some malls even provide places to sit and eat, which makes shopping more enjoyable for families and friends.

There are people who like to walk around the stores even if they do not buy anything because it feels relaxing.

Shopping can also be a way to celebrate events like birthdays or holidays with loved ones.
