For some people, shopping is not just about buying what is necessary. Sometimes, it is for clearing your mind, removing stress, enjoying spending money, and many other things.

But for others, they do not enjoy it at all. They hate waiting, trying on clothes, and spending money on unnecessary things. But for some others, including myself, I would rather go shopping for necessary items. Going shopping while not needing anything? Well, I would rather stay at home than go to the mall.

For others, they do enjoy going to the mall as a form of entertainment or just to have something to do in their free time.

Men, have you ever seen a man or father having fun in the mall? Well, maybe. It is rare to find one out of hundreds who is happy no matter how long you wait. But for others who get mad from waiting, going shop by shop, and doing nothing, it is frustrating. Others go with friends but end up sitting more than shopping, mostly doing nothing but talking and laughing.

In conclusion, shopping may or may not be an entertaining activity. In the end, sometimes you are going for necessary things whether you enjoy it or not. But for me, I do not like to go to the mall for unnecessary things. I get bored and feel like I am wasting my time on things I would rather not do for fun. Some people go just to eat at the food court and do not care about the stores at all, which is strange but does happen.
