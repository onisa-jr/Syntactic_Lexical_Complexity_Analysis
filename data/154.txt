Shopping is a form of entertainment. For some people, shopping is not just about buying what is necessary. It is a way of treating oneself, a source of happiness, and a luxury.

Females most likely tend to go on a “shopping spree” just to treat themselves. For example, if a girl was having a rough midterm exams week, she promised herself to go shopping after her exams to treat and motivate herself. By “shopping spree,” I mean excessive shopping, going from one store to another, buying everything and nothing.

Shopping can be a source of happiness for some people. This means that a person might enter a mental state of sadness or depression, and the thing that can cheer them up is shopping. This can be makeup, a dress, or heels that a girl has been wanting for a long time. Sometimes, it is not about the product itself, but it is about the company. Shopping with friends and family is very enjoyable.

Shopping is a luxury that not everyone has. Some people are luckier than others. They have the privilege to buy anything they want whenever they want. Their lifestyle revolves around spending money just for entertainment. These people usually cannot feel good about themselves until they buy those designer brands. They place their value on these bags or other items.

Many people may argue that shopping is not entertaining at all. They believe that people only go shopping to get their necessities and then leave. To be honest, in this case, males are probably more likely to not enjoy shopping, and they may even go to the mall only once or twice a year. However, we cannot deny that most people actually find shopping entertaining.

In conclusion, shopping can combine both necessity and enjoyment. It depends on personality, gender, age, and many other factors. My point still stands: shopping brings joy to many people, especially because of how accessible it has become nowadays with online shopping.
