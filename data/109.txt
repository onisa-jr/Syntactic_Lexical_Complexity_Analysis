Some people may see shopping as spending money on things they need. Others may see it as something to enjoy and use to relax. For me, it is not just spending money on things I need. It is definitely also something that makes me feel good. This essay will discuss how shopping is a fun activity and why some people might not agree.

For me, shopping is something I enjoy for sure. It is not just about getting what you have to. It is the walking around and looking at what is there, the fun of going with someone or alone, the little stop for coffee or a drink, the trying on of clothes and touching of items, and the things you buy that you did not even plan to purchase. Now we can buy everything online, and everything comes to your door. But people still go to stores and shops, which shows that it is more than just buying.

Some people might not agree. They think shopping is just for when you need something. That way, you do not waste money or time. However, we surely need to do things that make us happy and spend time doing things just for ourselves.

In conclusion, shopping is not just about spending money. It is about the fun, the time, and the happiness you get. You can buy anything from home, but it would never feel the same. It is faster and easier, but it is not the same. So, shopping is not just something you do. It is something you like, and that is the reason we keep doing it.
