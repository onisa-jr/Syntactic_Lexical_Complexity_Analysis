Shopping is a process of purchasing and selling things, which could be clothes, groceries, jewelry, and various other items. Some people go shopping because they want to spend time, while others go shopping to buy necessities. Some people feel that shopping is a waste of time. However, with the advancement of technology and after the pandemic of coronavirus, online shopping has become a popular way of shopping. This argumentative essay will delve into why shopping is helpful to people by providing reasons and examples.

Shopping could be either online or by going to the mall or stores. A study revealed that shopping could reduce stress and lower the chance of getting depressed. When it comes to shopping at the mall, it is a better way of entertainment while maintaining your health. Online shopping has benefited people by reducing their time and effort. By clicking on whatever they want, it is now only a matter of time before what they have ordered is delivered. In addition, when the item is not as they expected, they can return it with just a click.

Many people argue that shopping is just a waste of time and effort. They have also claimed that online shopping is just a lie, that buying online costs more, and there is a large chance of being scammed. That can be true, but nowadays, laws and the police have even covered the online domain, so they will protect your rights. As mentioned above, shopping benefits you in various aspects.

To sum up everything that has been said, shopping is a form of entertainment that can also improve your health through walking and leaving the home. Online shopping makes it easier for people to purchase, which reduces time and effort. Some people have argued that shopping is not a form of entertainment and is a waste of time. However, that is not true for many reasons that have already been explained throughout this essay.
