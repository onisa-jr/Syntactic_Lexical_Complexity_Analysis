Shopping is to buy something you want or need. To buy something you need is more important, but is buying something you want less important? In my opinion, both are important, which means I agree that shopping is not only about buying what is necessary. It is necessary.

When we talk about what is “necessary,” I can only imagine what is needed to stay alive. For example, food, water, medicine, clothes, etcetera. A lot of things may be unnecessary for some people, but for others, they are. With shopping, I can buy something that I am completely aware I will not need or use, but I do buy it. Why? Because it looks cute. Sometimes, when I am stressed, I go shopping. It is far more entertaining and less stressful than buying only what is necessary.

There are a lot of people who think the opposite of my opinion. They say that it is a waste of time and money to buy unnecessary things. They only need and want what they truly need. For example, healthcare, food, water, something to wear, etcetera. Maybe there are a lot more, but those are the basic things that they think they need. When I think about it, I cannot stop myself from asking: is it really unnecessary? For me, everything is necessary—makeup, perfumes, etcetera. Everything is important to me. More than entertainment, it is a need for me.

In the end, everyone has their own opinion and thoughts about what to buy—whether it is necessary or not. Some think it is, and others think it is not. I think it depends on the person's thoughts about shopping. But for me, I agree that shopping is not only about buying what you need but also what you want. Last but not least, I hope everyone, at least once, treats themselves to something they “want,” not only to what they “need.”
