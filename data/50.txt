Shopping is something many people enjoy doing, but there are also people who do not like shopping unless they need something. I will explain whether I agree or disagree that shopping is for fun and not only for necessity.

Shopping is an activity that can make your mood better, and for many people, it is a favorite thing to do in their free time. I believe shopping helps people feel good and forget their problems for a while. For example, when someone buys a gift for themselves or for someone they love, it brings happiness and excitement. Also, some people say that shopping is like a hobby. It keeps them busy and gives them something to look forward to. Walking around in stores and looking at new things is also an enjoyable activity, and even if you do not buy something, the experience itself is pleasant.

However, shopping can become a problem if you are not careful with your money. If you spend too much only for fun, it can make you feel stressed later because you used money that you needed. That is why it is important to make plans before shopping and think about what you really need. People without enough money might feel bad when they see others enjoying shopping, so they may think it is not a form of entertainment.

In conclusion, shopping is something people do for fun but also for necessities, and if you balance it, it can be enjoyable. People who say shopping is only for necessities might not have extra money to enjoy shopping.
