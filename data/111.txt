Do some people go shopping just for entertainment? Shopping is mainly important for people and provides necessary and unnecessary items. For example, buying clothes for winter, like a jacket, is important. When the weather gets cold, we need suitable and warm clothes. For unnecessary things, like small items that are not important, some people buy them just for fun. In addition, some people buy items even when they already have multiple of them. Some people love buying unnecessary, expensive things because it makes them feel rich. I totally believe that there are people who take shopping as a form of entertainment. Moreover, people even admit the fact that they go shopping to have fun and that it makes them feel less sad.

I believe that shopping can be a form of entertainment. Some items, even when not necessary, are joyful and make an effort to make people happy. In addition, sellers benefit from it, and it is better in general for some people.

Although there are people who disagree, they find it a waste of money and not even fun to do. They believe that shopping should be done only for important things.

However, I claim that it is acceptable if people take shopping for fun, because it helps people feel happy. Of course, a person should be aware of where to use money, but that does not mean shopping should only be done when it is necessary.

In conclusion, people can do what they believe suits them and their lifestyle. Rich people can definitely take shopping as a form of entertainment because it suits their lifestyle and class.
