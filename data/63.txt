Some people believe that shopping is only to buy necessary things. However, this is such a basic way to see shopping. Since shopping is also a fun activity for most people, this essay will argue that shopping is an entertaining activity and not just for buying necessary things to keep you alive.

Shopping, to most people, is a fun activity where they can go with friends and family to buy goods and even have fun playing or eating out. For example, in malls, there are all kinds of fun activities, such as games and food places. Therefore, it is definitely a social and entertaining activity.

Lots of people enjoy shopping by themselves. They can go outside, try on clothes, test the food, and try everything before buying. This is a fun activity where they can clear their mind and enjoy being outside in nature. So, while it is a fun outdoor activity, it is also a way to make sure about what you are buying.

Some people think that shopping is only for necessary things, which is a boring way to look at it. With online shopping today, you can buy all the necessary things online without having to go outside at all. However, what a depressing idea it is to not go out and miss all the fun that shopping could provide.

In conclusion, shopping is also a form of entertainment. It is not just about buying the necessary things to keep you alive. It is also a social and outdoor fun activity, unlike what some people think.
