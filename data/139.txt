Who can ignore the shopping places? Only a few people, because shopping is something many people enjoy a lot. It is like something nice to do when you are feeling lazy or just want to go outside. Shopping is a fun way to spend your free day.

Some people go shopping together with their cousin or best friend because it makes the time more fun. Especially women, who can stay at the mall the whole day and not be bored even a little. They like to try on dresses or shoes, and sometimes they do not even buy anything, just try and smile. Looking at new things also makes them feel happy.

There is another kind of people who go shopping only when they have to. They do not walk around too much and just go where they know the thing is. They finish shopping very quickly and go back. This way is not exciting, but it saves time. Mostly it is the guys doing this — not every guy, but many of them.

The shopping mall is also a place where you can eat some snacks, drink coffee, and spend time with someone. It is not always about buying everything. A few people like to shop alone because they do not want to wait or talk too much. They enjoy being quiet and just looking slowly.

In the end, shopping is different for each person. Some love it and some do not, but maybe if you go with someone nice, you can enjoy it more. Shopping is also about the feeling, not only the things.
