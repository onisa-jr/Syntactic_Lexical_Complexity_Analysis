In recent days, shopping has become popular in various and different ways. Some people claim that the purpose of shopping is just to buy necessary things, while others argue that shopping is a form of entertainment and a hobby to escape reality. This essay will delve into how shopping can actually be a source of entertainment, help with mental health, and increase one's positive energy.

The purpose of shopping does not necessarily have to be buying, but also it could be for leisure. Some people, like myself, view malls and shopping centers as an escape room, due to forgetting everything around and focusing on how beautiful the colors of clothes, shoes, and products are. Spending money is painful at some times, but shopping without buying does not require spending it, and with that, one's financial stability is maintained.

While some find joy in shopping without the need to buy items, others argue that it is a waste of time and effort. While this concern is valid, shopping, like any other activity, has to be treated as a personal freedom. If one enjoys walking around and observing the place for entertainment, it is their own bubble to decide. Moreover, shopping methods today have developed, and one can shop online, saving the effort and buying items for fun, not only for necessary needs.

Furthermore, buying new things, whether they are necessary or unnecessary, gives a feeling of satisfaction and happiness. For a lot of people, buying things involves a sense of happiness, leading to increased positive energy. With that, one can consider shopping a form of entertainment.

In conclusion, life does not have certain rules to live by, and just like life, everything in life has no rules. Shopping is a way to escape reality, a source of happiness, and a way to keep the great spirit, which makes it a form of entertainment and pleasure.
