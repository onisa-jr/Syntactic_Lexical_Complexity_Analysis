Shopping can be considered as a way to give yourself a reward. Everyone needs to find a way to celebrate little wins every day. You can find a lot of ways to do that, but for me personally, I think the best way is by shopping. Shopping can be joyful either online or by buying from the store. It does not have a right way.

Shopping online can be more fun and more helpful. You can buy whatever you want, and you will have it delivered to your house. A lot of products can be available, and more importantly, you can see other people's opinions about the product before buying it. Shopping in person cannot provide that.

Shopping can help with improving your appearance and help you to find the right style for you. Many people only wear daily clothes without thinking if that type of clothing is right for their body type, or if it does the opposite by making them look bad or, more specifically, boring. It can be argued that spending time or money on shopping is irresponsible behavior. However, every person has a different way of thinking and how they would spend their money or time. We are all free to do what we would like to do.

In conclusion, we cannot decide how everyone should eat, drink, and how often they buy things. If you think shopping is a waste of time, that is your opinion. That is how different we are from each other. Shopping can feel like opening a present you deserved for surviving the week, the month, or whatever is going on in your life. You should try to give yourself a reward.
