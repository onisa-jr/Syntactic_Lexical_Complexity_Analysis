The long-term effect of shopping addiction is getting attached to the items they buy. They will never throw them away. This can result in their house being crowded and unlivable.

Other people say that shopping brings them happiness, and it is a way for them to blow off steam. However, not many people can control themselves. Some of them have kids, and if they do not control themselves, things will go too far. They will not be able to buy the necessary things for their kids.
