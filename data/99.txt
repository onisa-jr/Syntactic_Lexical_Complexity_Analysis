For some people, shopping is one of the ways to spend their free time, but for some reasons, this is wrong. This essay will talk about the reasons why people think this way, how it began, and why it is wrong.

The researchers found that most people like to shop anyway, no matter if they will use the item or not, and they think that this helps them to be happy. No one can disagree with that, but not in this way. This can lead to not having the amount of money that you want.

Shopping has become easier after the pandemic, so in this way, people took it as their hobby. In this way, they think that what they are doing is right, but this has become everyday shopping.

Also, they do it because they see other people doing the same thing, so they believe it is something they must do. Some people start shopping when they feel bored or do not know what to do, and then it becomes a normal thing. It is not for the need but for the feeling.

Although these reasons are few, this is becoming a world problem. Spending your money on things that you do not need is a punishment for yourself.

In summary, shopping for some people is a bad habit and can lead to losing your money. In my opinion, I do not agree that shopping is a fun way to spend my money on things that I do not need just to make me happy, and I think that you only have to take the things you want.
