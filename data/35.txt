Entertainment while shopping. Enjoying yourself while shopping is a good feeling. Shopping is not always for what is necessary. Therefore, some research finds that shopping or just going to the mall can give you the best feeling. It helps you know what suits you. Spending money is enough to give you a kind of entertainment.

For me, I agree. It is so boring to just buy what is necessary, or even not go shopping if you do not have anything necessary to buy. Sometimes I go out and I do not need anything, but I still buy something small to feel better. When you are alone and you shop, it feels like you are with people even when you are not. Also, the music in the shops and the lights make you feel excited.

Some may argue that shopping is not a form of entertainment. They claim there are a lot of activities for entertainment. However, shopping can be a form of entertainment. You shop and have a good time, so why should it not be a form of entertainment? If you will not buy what is necessary, then what is the fun if you do not spoil yourself? Even if you have no money, just looking at things and trying on clothes is fun. Some people go to the mall just to take pictures and eat snacks, and that is also shopping. You can laugh and talk with your friend while walking in stores. That is also fun.

In conclusion, shopping is a form of entertainment. Have fun, spend money on yourself, and it can change your needs. What other reasons could there be to indicate that shopping is a form of entertainment?
