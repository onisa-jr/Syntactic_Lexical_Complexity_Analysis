Shopping is an important thing in life. Some people see it as a necessity, and others take it as an entertaining thing to do in their free time.

However, we cannot deny that sometimes we enjoy the new things we have bought. Personally, I agree that it is entertainment. I can forget about all my problems and mute my head, as I am a person who loves fashion and designing new dresses.

On the other hand, there are people who might disagree and do not find any positive feelings while doing the shopping. Some people take it as a chore and only go if they have to. For these people, maybe they hate crowded places or have anxiety because of them. Because of that, they will never see it as a form of entertainment, unlike others who love it.

The reason I believe it is entertainment is that we can be more social and explore this side. Also, we can go out with some friends and make challenges with them.

Buying a new thing can really affect our mental health and make us feel better. People may try shopping as entertainment, give it a chance, and take it as a fun activity.

In conclusion, I can understand both those who agree and those who disagree. It is all about what the person thinks. For those who agree, they could be interested in certain kinds of shopping. Those who disagree do not care about it and just go when they have to.
