Why do people go shopping when they feel bored? In the old days, people used to shop only when they needed something. But now, with life becoming easier and money being spent more freely, shopping is not just about needs. In this essay, we will explain why shopping has become a way to pass time and feel better.

Shopping is something that people do when they go to stores or markets and get things in return for money. Today, shopping is not always for necessary things. For example, many people go to the mall to walk around and buy some things to feel good. Even if they do not need them, they still do it. This is because it makes people happy for a short time. In some cases, this can become a problem of wasting. Also, some people like to show what they have bought on social media. Then others feel like they must also buy. This continues and creates a cycle that is hard to break.

Like what we said before, doing something too much can create problems. If you keep buying without thinking, it will cause issues like losing money and having too many things. So the person must be careful and try to stop if it becomes too much. It is not good to always follow what we want.

To summarize, shopping is something old, but now it has changed with time. People use it for fun and to change their mood. But you should know when to stop before it becomes dangerous.
