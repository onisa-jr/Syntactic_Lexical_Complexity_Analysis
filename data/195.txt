Buying clothes is a necessary activity to do for the sake of your fashion.  
Many people agree that shopping is fun. But the main argument is: is going to the mall only for shopping, or also to entertain yourself? This essay will delve into this argument.

Personally, I find that shopping is fun. It is not only about buying new clothes; it is about changing your routine, even for three hours or less. Also, I am not going shopping by myself. I can meet with some friends or hang out with my sisters. We can do a lot of activities together, like going to the cinema after shopping, and eating together. So, I really believe that going to the mall is not only for shopping; you can have fun there.

However, some people might have a different opinion and believe that there is no fun in going to the mall. Some people see that shopping online is much better than going to the mall. There are several reasons. For some, they hate going shopping because they think they might waste their time. Others hate busy places, just like the mall. It makes them feel anxious and nervous.

In conclusion, going to the mall is not only for shopping; you can also have fun there and do various activities. However, some people see the mall as a place where you only shop and leave. Some others would prefer to sit at home and shop online in quiet and peace. In other words, not everybody thinks going to the mall is fun or worth the time.
