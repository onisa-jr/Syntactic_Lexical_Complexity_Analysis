Who can say no to shopping? Not many people can, because shopping is something that most people like very much. It is a good activity to do when you are feeling bored or just want to go out of the house. Shopping is the best way to spend your free time.

Many people go shopping with their friends or family because that makes them feel more excited. Especially girls, who can walk around the whole day and not feel tired from buying clothes or just trying them on. Even not buying anything still makes them happy because they just like to see new things.

There is also a type of people who only want to shop when they really need to. They do not waste time and only go to one or two places and buy what they need quickly. This kind of shopping is not fun, but it is useful. Mostly men do this kind of shopping, not all, but many.

Shopping can be like a hangout place. You can eat, talk, and shop at the same time. It is not only about buying things. Some people enjoy shopping alone. They feel relaxed and do not have to talk or wait for someone else. They look around and find good things in peace.

Finally, shopping is not the same for everyone. Some feel joy and others feel tired, but if you go with someone who enjoys it, you may change your mind. Shopping is not just buying; it is also about feeling good.
