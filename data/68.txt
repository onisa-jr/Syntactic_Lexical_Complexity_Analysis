Shopping has become an addiction for some people. It is mostly for buying what a person needs. Although it is for buying necessities in most cases, for some people, it could be an addiction or simply a hobby.

For some people, shopping has reached the level of addiction. In such cases, people who go shopping every day and claim that it is for what they actually need are, in fact, addicted. They use such an excuse to avoid acknowledging their addiction. These people are simply seeing shopping as an act of buying things that they do not need.

In addition, some people go shopping because they see it as a hobby. They often go shopping whenever they have free time. It can be for lifting their spirits or for having fun. It is a form of shopping that is not about buying what they need.

Some people will argue that it is, in fact, for shopping for something they need. However, my point is more valid, because if it is done as a hobby or an addiction, then it contradicts the idea that shopping is for buying what is necessary.

To summarize, shopping is usually about buying what a person needs. However, some people do it because they are addicted or do it as a hobby that they see as fun. It has been believed that the majority of people are like this, and the number is increasing. Others might disagree with this idea, but they do not see the full picture of how shopping is not always about needs.
