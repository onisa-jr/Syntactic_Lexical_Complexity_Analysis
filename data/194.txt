Shopping is not just for buying what is necessary. There are many people who go through stress or who have anger issues and take it out on shopping. However, there are studies that prove the claims written, and that people who have extensive shopping problems spend a lot of money on shopping.

However, spending money just to release all the built-up feelings is wrong in many ways. The people who do this sometimes do not have the privilege. First of all, some people do not have the money and might spend the only money in their bank account that will keep them going for the whole month.

Secondly, these are people who actually regret it when they cool down after spending an unnecessary amount of money on clothes, accessories, or even groceries, and many more items they do not actually need. It is said that doing it makes one feel much better, and some regret it.

On the other hand, there are people who actually do spend money for fun and are ready to spend more than 10,000 riyals on just entertainment, food, clothes, and even more. It is acceptable if they have the money for it. If it makes them feel good and have fun, then why not let them spend the money?

In my opinion, there is no harm in spending money on items that are not important or necessary. If it will make the person feel relieved and much better, then it is acceptable, even if it is just for fun or to have a great time. Sometimes, people should spend more money than usual on themselves.
