In this upcoming world, shopping has become a lot easier for anyone. It can be done within a couple of clicks, or you have the choice to go to the nearest mall to get what you desire. However, availability has made people addicted to buying with no specific reason or need, simply because they believe it would make them happier. This essay will shed light on some psychological reasons and facts about why shopping is not only to feed our necessity but also can bring joy too.

First, a very popular psychological theory called FOMO (fear of missing out) can explain the uncountable number of purchases. An individual can simply buy an item just for the sake of satisfaction that they did not miss any important thing, although they had never felt the need for it.

Another reason for this joy is that people simply feel related to those useless things. We can notice this in many people who love to collect limited editions of games or products of their favorite artist or character and always crave more and more.

Many people may argue that they will regret these purchases in a short time, but the issue is that even if they realize later how silly and nonsensical their actions were, this short-time feeling of happiness cannot be denied. Everyone means to do so, obviously, because they know they are going to feel better afterward. This leads to the point that it is really a sort of entertainment for many.

To summarize, people’s preferences differ. While some enjoy doing activities or trying new experiences, the fact that there are also those who enjoy going to the shops and mindlessly buying things not much needed should not be rejected. This is considered to be a hobby for numerous individuals to relieve themselves and add this exciting touch to their life.
