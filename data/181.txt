Shopping is something we might do daily or weekly to get things we need. However, to some people, it is a form of entertainment, which is something I agree with due to a lot of reasons that we will talk about in this essay.

Shopping is a sort of entertainment due to the flowing ideas and happy emotions that arise when planning a whole outfit. It can also be car shopping, where you get to look at different types of cars. Shopping can give you a sense of enjoyment because it fulfills your desires to buy or try new things.

However, some people would disagree that it is a form of entertainment due to the time it takes or the effort required to make choices while shopping. They view it as a waste of time and energy that could be used for something better. For some, shopping can be a hassle.

In summary, shopping is a form of entertainment for most people. While some may not agree, they also cannot deny how it could be entertaining, despite the time a shopping trip may take. Nor can they deny how emotions change when shopping for something you desire. One would definitely not care about how much time they have wasted, nor would they think of something better to do. Personally, shopping is a huge source of entertainment for me, where I can unwind and enjoy every moment without noticing the time spent doing it.
