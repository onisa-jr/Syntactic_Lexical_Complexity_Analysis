Shopping is a common thing that many people do. It is something that happens in every country. Some people go shopping when they need something, but other people go because they feel happy when they go shopping. This essay will talk about why people shop and whether it is for need or just for enjoyment.

Shopping is a normal part of life. Some people go shopping when they need food or clothes. But other people go shopping because they want to feel better. Sometimes they see something nice and buy it, not because it is important. For example, a girl can buy a bag even though she already has many bags at home. Or a man can buy a watch just because he likes the color. Some people go to the shopping center and walk around for a long time. They do not buy anything, but they feel good just looking. That is why shopping is not always about what you must have.

Other people do not think the same. They say shopping is only when you need something. Many old people think like this. Also, people who do not have much money think shopping is only for things like food, water, or clothes. They do not buy extra things. For them, shopping is not enjoyable, but only for living. That is why they do not like to spend money for fun.

In conclusion, shopping can be different for every person. Some do it for need, some for fun. I think shopping is not only for necessities. It can help people feel good too.
