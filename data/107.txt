Shopping is considered a way for people to spend their own money, but can it also be a way of entertainment? Although shopping is very popular among people, many individuals overlook this question and do not think about it deeply. People argue that shopping is entertaining because of the feelings of happiness it may give, feelings of renewal or feeling new, and the feeling of fitting in.

Happiness is a common reaction after a long day of shopping. Buying new items can make a person happy very quickly. Many argue that it is temporary happiness and that it will not last, but so are many other things in life. No one is promised to live tomorrow, so why can we not make the most of our life while we can? Sometimes, temporary happiness is needed to survive.

Shopping can also make someone feel new. Having a small makeover or changing your appearance from time to time can be entertaining. Changing your style and clothes or buying your friends some clothing can be fun.

Another reaction to shopping is the feeling of fitting in with the rest of the world. That also can give someone a feeling of joy. Sometimes, people will be seen as strange just because of the way they dress. On the other hand, people who constantly shop for new clothes that are trendy will feel like they fit in. Many argue that it is not a good thing that we need to spend money to be liked, but anything is better than people developing depression from constant bullying because of their appearance.

To conclude, although shopping is popular, there are different opinions when it comes to proving whether it is fun or not. Many argue that shopping gives feelings of happiness, feelings of renewal, and the feeling of fitting in.
