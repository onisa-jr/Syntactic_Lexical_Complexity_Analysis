People may argue that shopping is not just about buying items either online or by going to the mall. It is about their joy and a form of entertainment for themselves. Some people consider that shopping is like a reward or therapy for them.

Shopping is considered one of the most favorite things for females rather than males. Men prefer sports or other activities.

Some people say that women would pay any amount of money when they are shopping. But in this claim, they portray women as less important than men, generalizing the idea of shopping and ignoring their efforts.

Others may say that shopping could raise the dopamine levels in a human's body, because they feel happy and joyful when they think about it or do it.

In many countries, shopping is like a hobby for all ages, not just for the young or for women. Older people also like to shop when they feel bored or alone.

Shopping can also be a way to forget stress and other negative feelings that life brings to them.

Some friend groups plan to meet in malls because it is more fun, and they can walk, eat, and shop at the same time.

Online shopping also makes it easier for people to spend money while they are at home.

Sometimes, they buy things they do not really need, but it gives them some kind of enjoyment or hope.

Shopping may not be for everyone, but for many, it is not a bad thing. It is a good way to feel better in the moment.
