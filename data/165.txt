Have you ever thought about shopping on a stressful day? Or is shopping just a life necessity? This debate can have many people arguing over it, because it can mean different things to us. In this essay, I will discuss how shopping can be a form of entertainment. Shopping is a form of joy and entertainment for what it brings to us, from the excitement of new items to the satisfaction of being able to buy things.

Buying random new items and adding them to your collection can bring you a great deal of joy and be a source of stress relief. Many people tend to go shopping on their bad mood days, as it distracts them from their life events and helps them feel the rewards they deserve. Although some people would say, "How can wasting money be rewarding and not cause more stress?" In fact, part of your income should be dedicated to this activity. Thus, it will not be a burden.

Satisfaction and self-confidence may increase after shopping, and that is why people shop even when they do not need to. Some people find their value in materialistic objects and connect their self-esteem to shopping and being able to afford things. However, this is not the right way to value ourselves. Yet, it is the reality for many people living around us. This proves that shopping can be more than just a necessity or a complement. It is a practice that contributes to better life quality.

In conclusion, shopping is a healthy life practice for many people struggling with mental health and self-esteem. It can bring people pleasant and rewarding feelings and enhance their life attitude. Purchasing random items sometimes could simplify our complexity and serve as a stress relief. Try random item shopping next time on a gloomy day and observe the effect.
