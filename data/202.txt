Shopping, if not helpful, should not be done. When you already buy something before, then you do not need to buy it again. It is just wasting time and using money for no reason. Many people now buy online from their phones or computers, but still, many people want to go outside and shop in the mall. Some people say shopping is exciting and makes them happy. Other people only want to buy the things that are needed. In my idea, shopping should be only when you must buy something, not for passing time.

Shopping, if not important, is not needed. A lot of people are busy with jobs, school, or family. For me, shopping is not interesting if I do not want to get something. There are better ways to use time, like cooking, sleeping, or spending time with your loved ones. These ways are better for your life and feelings.

There are people who think shopping is something to enjoy. They feel happy when they go shopping. Maybe they believe it is a kind of entertainment. But I do not think shopping for fun is the best idea. When you do this, you use your time for nothing. You can do other things that can help you in the future. For example, you can watch a good movie, go walking, or do some drawing. Walking can help your body and heart. Drawing can help your thinking and imagination. Also, going to nature is a good way to feel peace.
