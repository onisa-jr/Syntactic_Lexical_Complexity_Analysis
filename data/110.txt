In these days, there are a lot of malls and even online shopping. Shopping can be necessary and at the same time for entertainment. For most people, they do it for fun.

Some people think that they do shopping only for what they need, and they buy and love shopping because they see that shopping is only for the necessary things. Not for fun, and not to take all your time just because you want to have fun.

In my opinion, shopping is most of the time for fun. I do it too when I need something necessary. I buy everything I need, and also for fun, because it changes my mood. It does not stress my head from thinking. However, shopping is the best thing we have and we do. If I do not have something to do, I go shopping. Even my energy is different when I do it. Many times I go with my friend to the mall, and we walk only, look, and talk, and it makes me happy. It is not always that I buy something, but just to be there is good because my feeling changes. Some people say it is a waste of money, but I say it is not, because a smile is more valuable than money.

In conclusion, I agree that shopping is for fun and also changes our mood. But a lot of people see that shopping is only for necessary things. Why do we not do both in one, for necessary and for fun? In the end, it is for you and for changing your mood.
