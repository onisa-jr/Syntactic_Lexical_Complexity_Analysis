Shopping can be much more than only buying things. For many years, it was known that shopping can bring happiness, especially for women. Shopping is a form of entertainment. This essay is going to argue that shopping is more than just buying what is necessary.

Shopping can bring with it many advantages. Going to do what you like can help you relieve stress. Therefore, for people who like shopping, they can take some time to relax and go to the mall. Shopping also can be a type of exercise where you will have to get out of your house and take some time to walk and get fresh air.

Shopping can also have disadvantages. Some people cannot focus on only buying what is necessary. For example, they will spend their money on things they do not need. Also, shopping can be time-consuming. It might take a lot of time to head to the market, where it might be crowded. Furthermore, it can make you lose more money than what you intended.

Some people may argue that shopping is time and money consuming. What they say is true, but they can avoid these things by keeping an order for the necessary things they need to buy and focusing on them. Therefore, they will not lose time and money and can have all the advantages mentioned above.

To sum up, shopping is much more than buying what is necessary. It can be an exercise that relieves stress. It can be time and money consuming only if you do not know how to keep an order of your goals. In the end, always take time to relax, and going shopping can be a great start.
