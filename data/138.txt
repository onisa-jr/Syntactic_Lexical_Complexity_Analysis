Some people may see shopping as buying what is necessary. Others may see it as a form of entertainment. For me, it is not just about buying what is necessary. It is definitely also a form of entertainment. This essay will discuss how shopping is a form of entertainment and why some people might disagree.

For me, shopping is a form of entertainment for sure. It is not just about buying what is necessary. It is the experience you go through and the people you meet, the little break for coffee and a snack, and finding the things you look for and wish for. Nowadays, you can buy whatever you want from home. Everything can be delivered to your house. However, people still go to the market or the mall for shopping, which proves that it is a form of entertainment.

Some people might disagree. They think that shopping is only about buying what is necessary. That way, you would save time and save money. However, we surely need time for ourselves and to spend money on ourselves and our happiness.

In conclusion, shopping is not just about buying what is necessary. It is about the entertainment, the experience, and the time that you spend on yourself. You can buy whatever you want from home, but the experience would never be the same. It is less work for sure and requires less effort and time, but it would never be the same. So, shopping is not just something you do. It is something you feel, and for this reason, we keep doing it.
