Sometimes spending money is purely for fun. There are people who only shop for necessary things and avoid the items they do not need. On the contrary, there are people who disagree with this and believe that shopping is a form of entertainment. I believe that spending money can be a form of entertainment.

Shopping can be a way or a method of stress relief for anxious people. It can also distract them from their hectic lives. I think that if a person has enough money and is well off, spending money for fun is not an issue at all. Someone can buy things just to add them to a collection. This can give them a feeling of fulfillment. Another person can enjoy buying gifts without occasions as a form of self-expression. This is all possible if this person is financially stable and has a tendency to consume.

However, some people disagree and believe that shopping should only be for necessities. Shopping for fun can promote capitalism and consumerism, and can harm the economy. They think that if someone shops and spends great amounts of money, it can be immature and insensitive to the poorer groups in society. They think buying too many things can make a person arrogant and egotistic.

In conclusion, shopping for new things can be a form of distraction to some individuals and a way to express their emotions. It can open their eyes to new things in life and help them learn about themselves and their communities. People can disagree, but money is made and earned to be spent. Whoever has the privilege to earn it should spend it and enjoy their life. Shopping can also help people develop a keen eye for art, which is a skill that can help in certain artistic careers such as fashion and beauty.
