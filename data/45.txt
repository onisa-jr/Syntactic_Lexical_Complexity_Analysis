Shopping provides a good and beautiful feeling. Shopping is sometimes so necessary. You can shop at the mall and online. Some people shop only for entertainment, and some of them shop only out of necessity for something they need. Shopping is allowed for everyone and everywhere. You can shop from your home and from another city. That helps many people.

Shopping for entertainment is bad and a big mistake. Entertainment is not only through shopping, because it can lead to financial losses and negative energy.

Shopping is not always entertainment. You can entertain yourself in other ways, such as by gaming or traveling. Some people disagree with me. They think happiness comes only from shopping. This concept is wrong. It is only momentary happiness.

Sometimes, when you buy too many things, it brings you negative energy because you do not need them or they are not appropriate for you. Some people buy things and then feel confused. They do not know what happened to them. They think that shopping gives them positive energy and is good for them. There is evidence for my point of view. Also, some people continue to shop even when they have no money, and this causes many problems in their lives and their families.

People should think before shopping and ask themselves if it is really important or if they are shopping just because they are bored or sad.

In conclusion, you can shop and entertain yourself, but only if it is necessary and if you really need it. You can also entertain yourself in many other ways.
