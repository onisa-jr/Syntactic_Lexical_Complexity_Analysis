Did you know shopping is not only for buying necessities but also a form of entertainment? Shopping is one of the most common activities in today’s world. Some people find it entertaining to buy things whether they need them or not. On the other hand, some people think it is a waste of time and hate even buying the necessities. Shopping is a good activity and has many benefits.

First, shopping is not only for buying what you need. It is good for mental health. According to many scientists, buying new things can make you happier. However, going to malls and stores that sell things you like can also help with mental health. Shopping with friends also makes the experience more fun, especially when you go in groups and try on clothes or buy gifts for each other. It is not just about spending money but also about making memories and sharing laughter. Sometimes people go to the mall just to walk around and not even buy anything, but simply enjoy the atmosphere and look at new things.

Besides, many people like shopping when they feel sad or bored because it helps them feel better. The color of new clothes or the smell of perfumes can change the mood very quickly.

On the other hand, some people hate going shopping and think it is a waste of time. They tend to use online shopping. They believe it is easier and better for them.

In conclusion, while some people love shopping a lot, others try to avoid it. But shopping is here to stay and will always be a big part of modern life.
