Shopping is not always about getting things that are needed, but for many people, it is a way to deal with stress or when they feel sad or angry. There are some people who just go to shopping malls to feel better or to stop thinking about their problems. Some studies say that shopping can help to reduce bad feelings in the moment and make people feel like they are doing something good for themselves.

Still, buying things just because a person feels something inside is not always a good idea. This is because some people do not have enough money, and they use the little they have for things that are not important. For example, someone might use their salary only for clothes or shoes, and then they do not have enough left for food or for paying important bills.

Also, when they calm down, they feel sorry about what they bought. They think they needed it, but later they realize it was just because they were upset or did not feel good. It is true that shopping can make someone feel happy, but not everyone feels happy after they do it, and some feel regret.

But there are also people who spend a large amount of money just for fun or because they enjoy it. Some people can use 10,000 riyals or more just for clothes or food, and they do not worry. If they have the money, it is their choice, and it is acceptable to enjoy.

In my opinion, spending money is not always bad. If it helps someone feel better or just to relax and enjoy, then there is no problem. People should sometimes spend more on themselves to feel happy and have fun.
