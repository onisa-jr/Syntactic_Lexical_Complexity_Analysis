Shopping is a great activity to do. Sometimes, people do think that shopping is just for buying what is necessary, but in my opinion, not all the time. Shopping is considered as entertainment as well. In this essay, I will argue against the statement.

First, let us talk about shopping in both ways. Shopping in general can reduce a lot of pressure, whether you are doing it for a necessary need or for entertainment. Some people hate doing it because it takes so much time, effort, and also money. Also, shopping can bring boredom and tiredness because it takes a lot of walking and choosing, and sometimes you do not find the things you are looking for. To others, shopping is a happy activity. That type of person would enjoy every moment of shopping. They would gladly give their time, effort, and money to fill the joy inside them. They would walk and pay for everything they want.

I disagree with the statement because I do not agree that shopping is only for necessary things. We could go and shop when we feel like we want to put our tension in another place. Sometimes, we could just go shopping in the mall to see the new things and buy what makes us happy when we feel sad.

Finally, not everything we do in life must be just necessary. Shopping does not mean we do it only for necessity, but also as a sort of entertainment. It feels good to buy something you want for yourself. In the end, not everything in life is only serious, but also you could do things to entertain yourself.
